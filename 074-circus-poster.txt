TITLE: The Grand Carnival
DESCRIPTION: An 1899 circus spectacular poster set on a dark wall: layered wood-type typography (Impact-narrow caps at multiple scales with offset gold and cream shadows), a scalloped bunting header, star-burst rosettes, a navy band, aged paper with twin vertical and horizontal fold creases, corner wear, and grain. Three acts — Herr Brutus the strongman, Madame Serpentina the wire-walker, Amazing Volta the contortionist — each carry a small framed vignette on canvas that plays its act in silhouette under a flickering spotlight when hovered or focused: Brutus pressing a bending barbell overhead, Serpentina swaying across the high wire with parasol above a safety net, Volta arching from bridge to folded box. A brass calliope button plays a self-synthesized oom-pah arpeggio loop, and a real tear-off ticket waits below the perforation to be ripped and reprinted.
TECHNIQUES:
- Wood-type poster typography: condensed system caps with scaleY condensation, hard offset text-shadows in cream and ink, letterspaced serif and mono small print — no web fonts
- Aged-paper treatment in pure CSS: radial edge-wear gradients, three fold-crease gradients (dark core plus highlight), and a two-scale dot grain overlay in multiply blend
- Inline SVG bunting generated at load (swagged catenary line with 22 colored pennants) and star-burst rosettes
- Three silhouette vignettes on canvas with a shared spotlight-and-sawdust stage: parametric figures animated by sine cycles (press cycle with barbell bend, rope sway with counter-tilting parasol, arch-to-fold interpolation with dashed box overlay)
- Vignettes animate only while hovered/focused (rAF started and cancelled on demand) with a static drawn frame under reduced motion
- WebAudio calliope: square-plus-detuned-triangle lead with a sawtooth oom-pah bass, scheduled ahead of the audio clock in a half-beat arpeggio loop; user-initiated toggle, suspend on stop
- Tear-off ticket: CSS perforation with punched holes, rip animations (body rotates and falls away, stub stays), reprint reset, keyboard operable
INTERACTION:
- Hover, focus, or click an act row to spotlight it and play its vignette on the poster itself; the spotlight ring and row tint respond, and a mono hint appears
- The brass CALLIOPE button winds the organ — a jaunty two-bar arpeggio with oom-pah bass loops until pressed again (never autoplays)
- Click or keyboard-activate the ADMIT ONE ticket to tear it along the perforation; the body rips away and a PRINT ANOTHER TICKET reset appears
- All act rows are focusable buttons with visible focus rings; the whole poster scales fluidly from 360px to 4K; reduced motion keeps a calm static vignette and an instant fade instead of the rip animation
