TITLE: FORMICA
DESCRIPTION: A living ant colony rendered as a soil cross-section. Warm-brown strata, stones, a sand-labeled nest with chambers and tunnels on the left, and a pheromone field of teal #7fd1c0 that breathes and evaporates across a coarse grid. Up to 240 three-segment ants leave the nest, probe the scent field with three antennae samples, find crimson crumbs, and haul them home laying trail — until evaporation prunes the weak paths and one emergent highway remains. A naturalist console keeps the census, tunes population, evaporation and urgency, drops feasts, and hides the trail layer for blind observation.
TECHNIQUES:
- Pheromone substrate on a 6px Float32 grid with exponential evaporation, rendered to an ImageData layer and upscaled with smoothing for a soft glow
- Stigmergy simulation: foragers steer toward the strongest of three forward pheromone probes; carriers dead-reckon home while depositing; food piles are crumb lists consumed one by one
- Static soil layer pre-rendered once to an offscreen canvas: strata bands, speckle, stones, quadratic-curve tunnels, elliptical chambers, mound glow and engraved label
- Three-segment ellipse ants with heading-aligned bodies and carried crumbs; drop-tag announcement, synthesized drop blip, census stats at 4Hz, capped dpr for 60fps
INTERACTION:
- Click the soil to drop a 26-crumb cache (shift-click or Feast for 70); scouts find it, trails strengthen, and competing routes visibly argue before one highway wins
- Sliders tune population (20-240), evaporation (glacial to amnesiac) and urgency (0.5-2x speed); Trails (T) hides the scent layer; Pause (Space) freezes the colony mid-step
- New colony (N) floods the nest; the census tracks foragers, haulers, piles, crumbs and peak trail strength; every action confirms with a toast
