TITLE: VENATION
DESCRIPTION: A generative botany plate where leaves grow instead of being drawn. A space-colonization algorithm seeds ~780 attractor points inside a chosen leaf silhouette — maple (palmate polar formula), ginkgo (notched fan wedge), or fern (pinnate scalloped frond) — and veins grow step by step from the petiole toward hungry points, rendered as accumulating sepia, prussian, or verdigris ink on cream laid paper (#f4efe2) with a double-pencil silhouette outline, petiole stub, faint stipple of unconsumed attractors, glimmering growth tips, and full-page feTurbulence grain. Herbarium serif labels and Latin names sit in a left margin column with mono method notes (influence 34px, kill 5px, step 2.4px). The signature moment: touching the paper scatters new attractor points and nearby sleeping nodes wake — the living venation visibly re-routes and forks to meet your finger in hypnotic slow motion.
TECHNIQUES:
- Space-colonization venation: per-tick attractor queries via a spatial hash grid (cell = influence radius), weighted direction sums (1/(d+4) falloff), 2.4px child nodes, kill-radius consumption, staleness deactivation, 5,200-node cap
- Procedural silhouettes with inside(x,y) tests: maple r(θ)=0.60+0.40·|cos2.5θ|^0.65 + serration; ginkgo cosine-power wedge with apical notch; fern sine-envelope with 21-cycle pinna scallops — outlines traced by 22-step bisection along 440 angles, double pencil pass
- Layered rendering: persistent offscreen ink canvas (round-capped segments, width 2.7·(1−len/max)^1.35+0.45 tapering from base) composited over a paper layer with silhouette, plus live attractor stipple and tip glow each frame
- Touch-to-reroute: new attractors wake done-nodes within 2.6·Di² so growth resumes toward the touch; coalesced pointer events for smooth painting
- Growth-speed slider (0–14 ticks/frame, halved under reduced motion), species segments, ink swatch re-inking of all existing segments
- Tabular mono counter (iteration / nodes / attractors / vein mm at 3.78px = 1mm), italic serif annotations, radial paper sheen
INTERACTION:
- Watch the leaf grow from its petiole — veins branch toward the margins over the first ~1,500 iterations
- Touch or drag across the paper to scatter attractors: veins wake, bend, and fork to reach your points
- Switch species (Acer / Ginkgo / Filix) for three different silhouettes and growth characters; each regenerates
- Pick sepia, prussian, or verdigris ink — existing veins re-ink instantly in the new colour
- Drag the Growth slider from ×0 (hold your breath) to ×14 (time-lapse); Regrow (or R / Space) restarts; the mono counter tracks every iteration
