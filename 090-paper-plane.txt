TITLE: Flight School
DESCRIPTION: A paper-plane physics playground in a blue-sky classroom yard. Grab the white dart, drag back, and release to throw — velocity scales with pull distance, an aim tether and ghost-trajectory preview guide your hand. The plane glides with speed-dependent gravity, rides placeable updraft fans in rising arrow columns, drifts with a wind slider (headwind to tailwind), and crumples into a paper ball on hard landings. A dotted chalk-blue path draws every flight across the sky; the ground is ruled in meters with a grass edge; a wooden plaque keeps the best distance; mode chips track Ready / Aiming / In flight; and toasts narrate records and crashes.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas playground: gradient sky with soft sun glow, drifting multi-lobe clouds, grass ground with sine-blade texture and meter tick rulers every 10 m
- Throw physics: pull vector to launch velocity (260–780 px/s scaled by power), speed-dependent gravity for glide feel, orientation eased toward velocity vector, wind acceleration, and ceiling/wall bounce
- Updraft fans: placed columns with gradient shimmer, animated rising arrows, and radial lift applied to planes passing through, drawn as wooden base units with a fan grill
- Dotted flight path recorder (1.5/7 dash pattern) with launch-point dot; ghost-trajectory preview during aim using the same integrator
- Dart rendering as a two-tone folded paper plane (shadow wing, white wing, fold line, blue nose accent); crumple state as an irregular polygon with crease strokes
- Poster-style header with wooden record plaque, press-down console buttons, custom wind slider, and mode indicator chips
INTERACTION:
- Grab the plane and pull back — a red dashed tether, a growing power ring, and a blue ghost arc preview the throw; release to launch (short pulls cancel)
- Place up to five updraft fans by activating the Place updraft button and clicking the yard; planes passing through get lifted — the signature long slow arc comes from throwing into a well-placed fan column
- The wind slider (-0.4 to +0.4) changes drift live; headwinds shorten flights, tailwinds stretch them
- Hard landings crumple the plane into a ball with a toast; gentle landings let it skid to rest; distance is measured against the meter-marked ground, updating Last throw and the Best distance plaque with record toasts
