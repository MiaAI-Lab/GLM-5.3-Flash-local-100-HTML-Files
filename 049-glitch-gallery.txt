TITLE: SIGNAL LOST
DESCRIPTION: A dark control-room gallery of six corrupted transmissions. Each frame is a live Canvas 2D generative broadcast — a dimmed SMPTE test card, a weather-balloon horizon, a night traffic cam, an interview with the subject withheld, an open-water buoy feed, and an unknown oscillator pattern — degraded in real time by a post-processing pipeline: RGB-split tint bands, datamosh block-shifts that copy rectangles of the frame to new positions, scanline tearing, dropout sparkle and dashes, and a chroma wash at high corruption. A VHS OSD (PLAY, channel, counter, CORR%) floats over every frame, the whole gallery re-seeds its corruption periodically, a global degradation slider scales the damage, and pressing G launches a full-frame glitch storm.
TECHNIQUES:
- Six generative source scenes drawn to offscreen buffers each frame (procedural buildings with lit windows, sine-wave seas, Lissajous oscillator grids, silhouette portrait with rim light)
- Post-FX pipeline sampling the frame's own canvas: drawImage self-copies for datamosh blocks and scanline tears, additive 'lighter' channel-tint bands for RGB aberration, speckle dropout, overlay chroma wash
- Per-frame corruption level with eased approach toward a target (global slider + hover boost + storm), and scheduled seed rotation so every visit decodes differently
- VHS OSD styling: white mono with magenta/cyan text-shadows, letterspaced small caps; broken display title with clipped chromatic ghost layers and a scanline-stuffed dead letter
- Chromatic-aberration header OSD line with live master counter and clock
INTERACTION:
- Hover or keyboard-focus any frame to escalate its corruption (+38%)
- Drag the global degradation slider (0–100%) to set the room's baseline damage; readouts update per frame
- Press G (or click Glitch Storm) to ramp a full-gallery storm to maximum for ~2.6 seconds, then relax; holding G keeps all frames escalated
PROMPT:
Design SIGNAL LOST: a near-black control room (#050505 base, faint static speckle field) dressed in VHS OSD language. Header: a mono OSD line "SP · ▸ PLAY · 0:00:00 · CH 049 · --:--" with a live ticking counter and clock; below it a huge broken display title "SIGNAL LOST" where the O is stuffed with horizontal scanlines (background-clip:text) and ghost copies of the whole title split into magenta (#ff2bd6) and cyan (#26f7fd) layers offset ±3px with partial clip-paths; then a dim mono subtitle about six transmissions recovered from a decommissioned relay.

Below, a control rack: a "Global Degradation" slider whose track gradients cyan→magenta with a white thumb and % readout, a "Glitch Storm" button (pressed state glows magenta/cyan), and a hint "press G · hold to escalate".

The gallery: six frames in an auto-fit grid (min 300px). Each frame is a 640×400 canvas with its own generative scene — CH 01 a dimmed SMPTE test card with identity circle and "KX-049 · IDENT"; CH 02 a dusk-gradient horizon with setting sun, mountain ridge and a drifting weather balloon labeled "ALT 32 400 m"; CH 03 a night city of procedural buildings with flickering amber windows and a passing red car on the road, "CAM 12 · JCT 9"; CH 04 a spotlight interview silhouette "[WITHHELD]" with blinking REC dot; CH 05 an open-water buoy at dusk with layered sine waves and a flashing light; CH 06 a black screen with breathing magenta/cyan Lissajous patterns "PATTERN 12 · ORIGIN UNKNOWN". Overlaid OSD in white mono with chromatic text-shadows: "▸ PLAY" top-left, channel top-right, running counter and "CORR NN%" bottom. A caption bar under each frame gives its title plus a rotating seed number ("seed 049-XXXX") that re-rolls every few seconds.

Levels ease toward target so corruption blooms and relaxes smoothly; hovering a frame adds +38%; the storm ramps everything to 100% for 2.6 seconds. Master footer in mono caps: "relay KX-049 · tapes unbaked · heads demagnetized last spring / format VHS SP · dropouts compensated · nobody is transmitting / tracking… NN%".
