TITLE: CELESTIAL ATLAS
DESCRIPTION: An antique star atlas plate from 1897, laid on a dark table under lamp light: parchment (#efe6cf) with deckled torn edges and faint foxing stains, a fine coordinate grid (longitude every 30 degrees, declination +40 to -40), a gold-tipped compass rose, an engraved title cartouche ("The Winter Circle — Orionis familia"), and a red wax observer's seal. The signature moments: hovering a constellation inks its figure glowing gold and slides in a parchment myth card with real lore and star facts; and a brass loupe with a turned handle follows the cursor everywhere, magnifying the actual plate underneath at 2x.
