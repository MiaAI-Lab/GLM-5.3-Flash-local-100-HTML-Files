TITLE: Drip Studio
DESCRIPTION: A white-gallery wall where paint pours with real gravity: pick a pigment pot (cadmium red, ultramarine, chrome yellow, viridian, titanium white, ivory black), then hold the cursor on the canvas to pour — a widening stream feeds ropes of paint that run down the canvas, wander, split at high flow, decelerate with viscosity, and dry from glossy bulbous heads to matte trails. A viscosity slider retunes every run's speed and fattening; a varnish sweep lays a skewed glossy sheen across the whole painting; "new canvas" crates the finished work with a springy slide-in crate stamped FRAGILE. Museum wall label and a tiny mono accession line complete the room.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas drip simulation: per-run ropes with viscosity-dependent speed decay, sine wander, flow-driven splitting, tapering stroke segments, and elliptical glossy heads with specular white highlights
- Persistent painting surface (base coat painted once, trails drawn per frame over it, cleared only on new-canvas) so drips accumulate like real paint
- CSS paint pots with pip-code labels and "in use" stamping; custom sliders with museum-gray tracks
- Varnish as a skewed translucent sheen band animating across the canvas in screen blend mode, plus a warm canvas tint
- Museum dressing: taped wall label (artist/title/year), accession number, live wet-run counter, and a wooden crate that stamps in on crating
INTERACTION:
- Hold the pointer on the canvas to pour; move while holding to braid multiple runs; release to stop
- Choose pigments from the shelf; each pot loads the cursor bottle and starts a new pour number
- Viscosity slider retunes run speed, head size and wander (thin wash to heavy gel); pour volume scales spawn rate and splitting
- Varnish sweep adds gloss; Force dry mattes all wet heads; "New canvas" crates the painting (click the crate to repeat) and stretches a fresh surface
