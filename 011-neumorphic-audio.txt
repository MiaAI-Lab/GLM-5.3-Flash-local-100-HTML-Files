TITLE: SOFTWAVE
DESCRIPTION: A neumorphic ambient-sound mixer presented as a soft, extruded instrument panel on a #e3e9f1 body. Four circular pads (Deep Tide, Glass Chorus, Soft Rain, Slow Pulse) sit in a deck of raised cards; each pad puffs gently and glows moss-to-blue when active. Below, a raised console houses an inset oscilloscope with a soft ghost trace, three draggable rotary knobs (Tone, Drift, Shimmer) with conic-gradient tick arcs, and a vertical master fader in an inset slot. Dual light/dark shadows (#ffffff / #bfc7d5) define every element; the accent blue is #6c7ee1 with a moss #7da88b secondary. System-ui medium typography, soft small-caps labels, and mono readouts complete the calm, tactile identity. WebAudio synthesizes layered ambient drones — detuned sines and filtered pink-ish noise — that audibly morph as the knobs turn while the scope draws live analyser data.
TECHNIQUES:
- Neumorphic UI: paired light/dark box-shadows (--raise / --sink custom properties) extruding and inset on a single #e3e9f1 surface
- WebAudio synthesis: detuned sine stacks with per-oscillator detune LFOs, a pink-tinted noise buffer through a bandpass filter, and a slow-pulse amplitude LFO, all summed through a master low-pass filter, gain node, and AnalyserNode
- Conic-gradient knob arcs masked with radial-gradient rings, driven by a --val / --sweep CSS custom property pair
- Oscilloscope drawn on Canvas 2D from getByteTimeDomainData with dual traces, glow shadowBlur, and a baseline fallback when silent
- Pointer-capture drag interactions for knobs and fader with full keyboard equivalents (arrows, Home/End, Shift for fine steps)
- Pointer-events-clean layered scope: an inline SVG ghost waveform beneath the live canvas
INTERACTION:
- Click the power pill (or press Space) to wake the audio context; the label flips from Standby to Live and a moss LED lights
- Click any pad (or press keys 1–4) to toggle that layer; the circular pad puffs with a breathing animation and an inset core turns to a blue-moss glow
- Drag knobs vertically (or focus and use arrow keys) to morph cutoff, detune spread, and noise brightness audibly; values shown in mono readouts
- Drag the master fader (or use arrow keys when focused) to ride the overall level, with a fill gradient tracking the value
- Hover states lift cards and dials; active states sink them; focus rings are soft blue halos; reduced-motion calms all breathing and animation
PROMPT:
Design "SOFTWAVE", a neumorphic ambient-sound mixer, as one self-contained HTML page (no scripts beyond one inline script tag, no external assets). Body background #e3e9f1 with two soft radial highlights; every surface is modeled purely with paired box-shadows — light from the top-left (#ffffff) and shade to the bottom-right (#bfc7d5). Define custom properties: --raise for extruded elements, --sink for inset wells, plus small variants. Typography is system-ui at weight 500/600: uppercase labels with .24em tracking at .66rem, mono (ui-monospace) value readouts at .68rem, and the brand headline "SOFTWAVE" letterspaced .34em. A small animated equalizer mark of three gradient bars sits beside the title. The masthead also holds a raised pill power button with an LED dot that turns moss green when live. Main content: first a deck of four raised cards (auto-fit minmax(206px,1fr) grid), each with a circular pad face (radial-gradient highlight, raised), an inset circular core, a small-caps name (Deep Tide, Glass Chorus, Soft Rain, Slow Pulse), a one-line whisper description, and a mono keycap (1–4) in the corner. Active pads puff with a slow scale animation and gain a soft blue ring glow. Below, a large raised console with four corner screws contains an inset oscilloscope panel (min-height 236px) with a faint SVG ghost sine beneath a live Canvas 2D trace drawn from AnalyserNode time-domain data: a dim moss trace at half scale under a glowing blue main trace, over faint grid lines. Beside/below it sit three rotary knobs — Tone, Drift, Shimmer — each 96px dials with a conic-gradient progress arc masked to a ring, a raised rotating body with a glowing indicator dot, mono value under the small-caps label; drag vertically to change, arrows for keyboard. A vertical master fader (52px track, inset well, 46px rounded thumb with grip lines) completes the console; on narrow screens it stacks below. Audio: on first user gesture create an AudioContext; build four togglesable layers — detuned sine stacks (55/110/220 Hz with slow gain LFO), a glassy five-partial chord with detune LFO, pink-tinted noise through a bandpass, and a 2.4s amplitude-pulsed fifth — all through one lowpass filter (180 Hz–9 kHz exponential Tone mapping) and master gain tied to the fader. Knobs morph filter cutoff, per-oscillator detune spread, and noise brightness with setTargetAtTime ramps. Power off ramps gain to silence and suspends the context after a delay. Canvas resizes with devicePixelRatio, animation loops via requestAnimationFrame with cleanup on pagehide, keyboard Space toggles power, an aria-live status region announces changes, and prefers-reduced-motion stops breathing animations. Escape ampersands in copy; quote every attribute; no lorem ipsum — real, quiet, evocative microcopy about tides, glass, rain, and lighthouses.
