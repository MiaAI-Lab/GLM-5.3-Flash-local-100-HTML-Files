TITLE: PRESS ROOM
DESCRIPTION: A risograph poster lab pinned to a dark pressroom wall. The centerpiece is a slightly crooked cream sheet bearing a heavy grotesque poster — SLOW WIRE, an evening of drones and shortwave choir — with a duotone halftone portrait rendered live on canvas from riso blue and fluorescent pink dot screens, misregistered on purpose. Crop marks, paper grain, worn edges and a mono print-spec footer sell the print-shop fiction. A press console offers registration, screen ruling, screen angle and paper grain sliders, three ink pots, subject switching, a misfeed randomizer, and pressure-sensitive dot breathing when the sheet is held.
TECHNIQUES:
- Live halftone rendering: a rotated dot grid sampled against an analytic luminance field (parametric face with key-light shading and a bowed seated figure), dot radius = pitch × sqrt(coverage), three ink tones per drum
- Deliberate misregistration: each ink pass offsets opposite-signed X/Y shifts scaled by the registration slider, with multiplicative blend of translucent dot layers
- Paper simulation: CSS layered repeating-linear-gradient grain, inset vignette, radial highlight, plus canvas grain speckles scaled by the grain slider
- Misregistration on display type via CSS ::before ghost copies (data-t attributes) offset by the drums
- Pressure interaction: pointer distance from sheet center drives dot size; ambient slow sine "drum breathing" keeps the screen alive at 60fps
INTERACTION:
- Drag the Registration slider (±9 mm) to slide ink layers from perfectly aligned to deliciously sloppy; the spec footer narrates ("trims honest" → "deliciously sloppy")
- Tune screen ruling (14–80 lpi), screen angle (0–45°) and paper grain; toggle Blue / Pink / Key ink pots; switch Portrait / Figure plates
- "Wipe Drums" clears all ink; "Misfeed" scrambles registration, angle and ruling for instant happy accidents
- Press and hold the sheet: dot size breathes with pointer pressure; keyboard focusable sliders with pink dashed focus rings
