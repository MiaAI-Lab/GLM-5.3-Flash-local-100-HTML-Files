TITLE: Gravity Works
DESCRIPTION: A hand-built marble run on a warm workshop pegboard. A wooden feed hopper sits at the top center; glass cat's-eye marbles pour from it onto planks you draw peg-to-peg, ring brass bumpers you stamp on any peg, and bank at a dark timber floor with a satisfying thud. Gravity and bounciness dials reshape the whole run live; every peg and bumper strike plays a pentatonic chime (higher notes up the board) that accumulates in a melody ribbon on the bench. Palette: pegboard #e8e2d4 with #cfc6b4 holes, wood #b98a55/#8a6540, brass #c9a227, six marble colors.
TECHNIQUES:
- Impulse-based capsule collision: marbles resolve against chute planks via closest-point-on-segment pushout with restitution, against pegs via nearest-grid-neighbor circles, and against bumpers with high restitution and glow pulses
- Chime engine: pentatonic C-major scale (C4-A5) mapped to board height, triangle/sine partial pairs for bright bumper hits vs soft peg ticks, rate-limited per marble; floor thuds from lowpassed noise
- Two physics substeps per frame with capped dt for stable stacking; per-marble fading trails; dpr-capped canvas at 60fps
- Workshop set dressing: pre-rendered pegboard with 3D holes, ruler edge, stenciled labels; gradient wooden hopper funnel with FEED stencil; wooden planks with brass end nails; glassy marble rendering with cat's-eye ribbon and specular
INTERACTION:
- Tools: Chute (drag peg to peg), Bumper (stamp brass on a peg), Erase (tap to pry off planks or bumpers), Hand-drop (tap to place a marble), Pour (hold the button to stream from the hopper), Foreman's run (stamp a proven zigzag and auto-pour four marbles), Strip (clear all)
- Gravity (0.2-2 g) and Bounciness (10-100%) dials reshape the run live; the melody ribbon grows one brass dot per note with size mapped to pitch, capped at 14 visible
- Keyboard: 1-4 tools, H hopper drop, P foreman's run, C strip, M sound; footer tracks pegs, active, banked, peak speed and session time; cap of 26 marbles keeps 60fps
