TITLE: CAFÉ DE FLORE
DESCRIPTION: A Parisian café menu and ordering card on aged cream paper inside a gold double-rule frame. The masthead pairs gold art-nouveau fleurons with a Didot-feel serif wordmark; the menu card carries four gold corner flourishes, two courses of dotted-leader items in serif with italic garnish notes, and an espresso cup with continuously rising steam beside a handwritten epigram. A green-framed chalkboard (handwritten cursive script font) lists Marcel's daily suggestions. Everything clickable flows onto L'Addition — a sticky mono receipt with a perforated top edge, table number, live Paris clock, item lines whose dotted leaders animate in like a dot-matrix tab, a 15%-service note, and a big oxblood PAYÉ stamp that thunks down rotated when the bill is settled. Clicking a receipt line removes the item; clicking Payer again resets the table.
TECHNIQUES:
- Art-nouveau corner flourishes and masthead drawn as inline SVG bezier vines in gold, mirrored via CSS transforms for all four corners
- Dotted-leader menu economics (flex nom / dotted rule / tabular-numeral price) reused across menu, chalkboard and receipt, with a clip-path "tab-in" animation on receipt leaders

- Receipt mechanics: a Map-tallied order rendered to DOM lines, quantity aggregation, cent-exact formatting, and a two-stage PAYÉ state machine with a scale-and-rotate stamp keyframe plus WebAudio thunk (low sine sweep + noise burst)
INTERACTION:
- Clicking any menu or chalkboard item adds it to the receipt (clicking again increases quantity; the item earns a red checkmark); clicking a receipt line removes it and the totals retally instantly
- Payer l'addition stamps PAYÉ across the receipt with a physical thunk sound and disables ordering; the button becomes "Nouvelle table" and resets the sheet
- The Paris clock updates in the receipt header and the footer; the espresso steams continuously
