TITLE: Penrose Paradox
DESCRIPTION: An Escher-homage study plate: an impossible triangle built from three marble beams drawn in axonometric projection, its lie manufactured honestly — every face is shaded from a single implied window, but the painter's draw order rotates by sector so the beams interlock the way they physically cannot. The object idles in a slow turn, winds through a full rotation as the page scrolls, and can be grabbed and spun with momentum. A lone vermilion figure walks the top faces forever, bobbing and striding around the loop; engraved paradox captions below (Fig. 1 The Lie of the Angles, Fig. 2 Three Truths No Whole, Fig. 3 Endless Ascent) take turns highlighting as the rotation passes through their sector. Mono OSD readouts report rotation and pitch; corner ticks, plate number, paper grain and a double-ruled stage complete the engraving.
TECHNIQUES:
- Axonometric 3D on Canvas 2D: three prism beams (4 side faces + 2 caps each) projected with a spin/pitch rotation matrix, per-face average depth for painter's sorting
- The impossibility engineered in the render order: a spin-sector lookup permutes which beam is painted last, so the depth lie rotates with the object and survives the full turn
- Faux marble per face: clipped linear-gradient tone by face orientation (bright top, dark soffit, mid sides) with beam-identity tint nudges and hand-drawn sinuous vein strokes
- Vermilion walker: a parametric figure positioned along the three top faces with stride and bob sines, scale-matched to the projection
- Engraved-plate art direction: double-ruled stage, corner ticks, paper-grain multiply overlay, mono OSD with tabular numerals, caption columns that light up by rotation sector
- Interaction model: passive scroll accumulates a wind parameter eased into the spin, drag adds direct spin with momentum on release, pitch tilts by drag height; caption auto-scroll on click
INTERACTION:
- Scroll the page to wind the triangle through orientations that could never exist — the shading quietly betrays the lie at each sector change
- Drag horizontally to spin the object directly (with momentum on release); drag vertically to tilt the pitch; the OSD reports live rotation and pitch in degrees
- Click the stage (without dragging) to smooth-scroll to the caption belonging to the current sector; caption callout lines rewrite themselves as their sector arrives
- The tiny vermilion figure walks the endless stair forever, even while you spin the world beneath it
- Reduced motion: the idle turn stops, the figure still advances subtly, and all interactions remain available
