TITLE: WINGS OF TIME
DESCRIPTION: A virtual gallery wing where scrolling walks you down a long plaster corridor hung with six living generative artworks — a particle flow field, interfering ring moiré, two cellular automata (rules 30 and 90), and two- and three-source wave interference — each updating live inside its frame. The fixed canvas scene pans with scroll: oak floor (#b98a55) with plank seams, marble-like reflections of every glowing frame beneath the floor line, spotlight cones from a ceiling track with #fff3d6 pools that brighten on whichever work you approach (the curator's light), bench silhouettes with soft shadows, painted room numerals I/II, an EAST WING entrance mark, and a guest desk with an open book at the end. Serif didactic cards (title, artist, medium, year, one-line note) hang beside each piece. The signature moment: reaching the end slides up a guest book — sign it and your entry is inked into a persisted archive.
TECHNIQUES:
- Scroll-driven camera: 760vh runway maps scrollY to camX with eased lerp; fixed canvas repaints the corridor each frame (wall gradient, baseboard, ceiling track, oak floor with seams, radial vignette)
- Six independent mini-artwork engines on offscreen supersampled canvases: flow field with fading trails, dual-ring moiré with orbiting centers, rule-30/90 CA scrolling via drawImage self-copy, and quantized wave-interference fields (2 teal sources, 3 violet sources) computed per 5px cell
- Museum dressing: spotlight cones (linear gradients), floor light pools (radial gradients clipped to floor), mirrored artwork reflections with gradient fade, didactic cards drawn with serif/sans canvas text and wrapText, brass edge accents
- Curator light: nearest artwork to viewport center gets boosted cone/pool alpha and reflection opacity
- Dust motes drifting inside the beams; progress bar, room readout, and nearest-work counter in the UI chrome
- Guest book: fixed card sliding up past 94.5% scroll, entries persisted in localStorage (guarded), serif who/italic say/mono when layout
INTERACTION:
- Scroll (or ←/→ keys, or swipe) to walk the wing; the camera eases and the spotlight pools glide with you, brightening around the nearest work
- Watch each frame breathe — particles flow, rings interference-shift, automata climb, ripples bloom — all live at all times
- Read the didactics beside every piece; the plate tracks ROOM and nearby work number
- At the corridor's end the guest book rises: sign your name and a line for the archive (persisted between visits); CLOSE to keep walking
- Reduced motion freezes the artworks' idle animation but the walk remains fully functional
