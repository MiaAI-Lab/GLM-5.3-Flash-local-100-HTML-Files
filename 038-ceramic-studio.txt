TITLE: Glaze & Fire
DESCRIPTION: A sunlit potter's studio built around a spinning wheel. A lathe-rendered clay vessel rises from a dark wheel head with splash pan and spokes; seven draggable pull points reshape the wall through a Catmull-Rom spline so every gesture reads as one smooth profile. Glaze chips — celadon, tenmoku, shino, or naked clay — tint the piece before the kiln panel ramps from 20°C to the glaze's cone with climbing, soaking and cooling phases, an ember-and-glow takeover of the whole stage, crackle and oil-spot formation, and a serif reveal card naming the finished opus. Studio palette: warm plaster #efe7db, clay #b4704f, wheel grays, kiln fire #b3402a.
TECHNIQUES:
- Lathe rendering: normalized radius/height control points smoothed by a Catmull-Rom spline into ~100 profile samples, drawn as shaded horizontal bands with slope-based lighting and a cylindrical sheen gradient
- Kiln simulation: eased ramp curve to the glaze's peak temperature, soak phase with crackle audio ticks, cooling decay, cone display (1-6 bending), ember particles and a full-stage color takeover toward ember orange
- Glaze character: crackle polylines and tenmoku oil-spot dots generated in vessel space after firing; per-glaze temperature, note and reveal naming from the final silhouette ratio (bowl / vessel / bottle)
- Spinning wheel chrome: rotating bat rings and spokes, contact shadow, wet-clay gleam, wheel hum and clay-squelch WebAudio synthesized from noise and saw/triangle oscillators
INTERACTION:
- Drag the amber pull points horizontally to swell or neck the wall (wheel hum rises with your speed); New lump deals a preset with jitter, Re-center straightens it; each completed pull increments the pull counter
- Pick a glaze chip to advance to step 02 and read its firing note; Fire the kiln (F) starts the ramp — the stage darkens, embers rise, cones bend, and the temperature readout climbs from 20°C to cone temperature (up to 1300°C)
- After the soak, Open door ends the cooling early; the reveal card names your piece (e.g. "Celadon bottle") with peak temperature, crackle count and opus number; New lump starts opus 2
