TITLE: DESCENT
DESCRIPTION: A scroll-driven voyage from the sunlit ocean surface to the bottom of the Challenger Deep, 0 m to 10,900 m. A fixed canvas paints the entire water column: its two-stop gradient is interpolated through six depth keyframes (from #7fd3e6 Caribbean light to #02060d hadal black), sun shafts flicker near the surface and die by 400 m, marine snow thickens with depth, and a darkness vignette closes in like a pupil. Twelve hand-drawn canvas fauna silhouettes — turtles, reef fish, pulsing jellyfish, glass squid, lanternfish, an anglerfish with a glowing lure, gulper eel, dumbo octopus, sea cucumber, hadal snailfish — each fade in only within 900 m of their true depth. A fixed depth gauge on the right runs a glowing bubble down a ticked track while a mono readout reports depth, pressure in atmospheres, surviving light percentage and temperature. Five glass chapter cards (Sunlight, Twilight, Midnight, Abyss, Trench) slide up and illuminate as the viewport centers them, each with a drop cap, real oceanography, and a fauna strip. prefers-reduced-motion replaces scrolling with a fixed depth slider.
TECHNIQUES:
- Scroll-position-to-depth mapping: window scroll ratio drives a smoothed depth value (0-10,900 m) that interpolates canvas gradient colors through six keyframed stops and controls light (exponential attenuation), pressure and temperature readouts
- Canvas 2D scene painting at devicePixelRatio: depth-keyed fauna silhouettes drawn procedurally (bezier fish bodies, radially-gradient jellyfish bells and anglerfish lure glow, sine-driven eel spines) with alpha windows around their true depths; sun-shaft polygons under screen compositing; marine-snow and plankton particle layers with capped counts
- Fixed chrome: right-rail depth gauge with programmatically generated ticks at 0/200/1000/4000/6000/10,900 m, a radial-gradient bubble positioned by transform, and a surface glint that fades out over the first 140 m via a CSS custom property
- Chapter cards light up by viewport-center proximity; drop caps via ::first-letter; backdrop-blur glass panels
- Accessibility fallback: prefers-reduced-motion hides the story, shows a fixed depth slider bound to the same depth state, disables shafts and smooth scrolling; rAF loop with pagehide cleanup
INTERACTION:
- Scroll (mouse wheel, trackpad, touch, keyboard) is the only engine — pure scrolling drives light, pressure, temperature, fauna appearances and chapter illumination from surface to trench
- Watch the depth gauge bubble ride down its ticked track while mono readouts tick upward in pressure and downward in light
- Cards snap to full opacity and cyan-edged borders when centered; each fauna species appears only within its true depth band, so creatures are discoverable by revisiting depths
- In reduced-motion mode, drag the fixed Depth slider to sweep the whole water column without scrolling
