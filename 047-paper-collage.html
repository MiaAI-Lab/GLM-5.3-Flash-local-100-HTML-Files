Torn & Assembled — Collage Wall No. 3

Everything on this wall was cut, torn or stolen: magazine caps, a receipt, the back of an envelope, one sheet of graph paper, a photo booth strip that kept its secrets. The cobalt thread is mine. Drag anything anywhere — the tape will complain a little.
