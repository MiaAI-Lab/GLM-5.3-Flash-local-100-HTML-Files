TITLE: SLEEP CAPSULE
DESCRIPTION: A futuristic capsule-hotel booking page for floor nine. A wall of twelve glowing capsule pods (bays A–F) each renders a live canvas interior — mattress, pillow, and a lighting-mode scene — behind a sliding privacy shade; clicking a vacant pod selects it (shade slides up, light warms, glowbar ignites), clicking again via Check-in occupies it. The console panel offers three lighting modes — Aurora (drifting teal-blue curtains), Starfield (twinkling gold stars, moon, occasional shooting star), and Ember (rose-amber glow with rising embers) — plus a mattress-temperature slider (18–40°C on a cool-to-warm gradient), sound-therapy toggles (Rain, Deep hum 52Hz, Night shoal), a quiet-hours lamp keyed to local time, and a check-in card summarizing pod, scene, climate, sound, and rate (¥4,800 · 22:00–10:00). The signature moment: switching lighting modes washes the entire page — selection color, glowbars, ambient radial wash, toasts, LED array — in that mood, because the room is the demo.
TECHNIQUES:
- Per-pod canvas scenes: animated aurora curtains (3 quadratic bands with sine phase), starfield (26 gold stars with twinkle, crescent moon via offset overdraw, periodic shooting star), and ember field (5 radial glows + 9 rising flickering embers), each finished with a mode-tint overlay
- Shade interaction: full-pod gradient shade translating up 46% with opacity fade on .selected (1s cubic-bezier), glowbar opacity/box-shadow ignition in the mode color
- Whole-page theming: body[data-mode] drives a --mode custom property consumed by wash (color-mix radial), selection, glowbars, LEDs, check-in accents, and toasts — all cross-fading over .6–1.2s
- WebAudio: looped pink-noise rain (lowpass 2100Hz with 0.06Hz swell LFO) / sub-bass 52Hz+78.2Hz hum with breathing gain LFO / night-shoal (lowpass 480Hz tide noise); node cleanup with gain ramp + disconnect
- Pod wall: auto-fill grid of bordered pods with hover lift, occupied pods dimmed with "OCCUPIED" ghost text, mono numbering, quiet-hours lamp (green during 22:00–07:00, amber outside), 15s hall clock
- Keyboard pods 1–9/0, aria-pressed toggles, half-rate scene painting for battery, reduced-motion calming
INTERACTION:
- Click a pod (or press its number): the shade slides, interior light warms to the current mode, and the check-in card fills with your pod
- Switch Aurora / Starfield / Ember — the pod scene repaints AND the entire page washes in that mood; toast confirms
- Drag mattress temperature 18–40°C: the readout, check-in card, and a warmth LED follow (past 34°C it lights)
- Toggle Rain / Deep hum / Night shoal — each is a distinct synthesized ambience, toggled off by re-click; clicking another swaps with a ramp
- Check in to occupy the pod (ghosted, unavailable to the next guest); three pods start occupied; quiet-hours lamp and hall clock track local time
