TITLE: GATSBY GALA
DESCRIPTION: An Art Deco gala invitation in gold-on-black. Stepped skyscraper silhouettes with lit windows rise behind the card; a slowly rotating sunburst of gold rays ringed with jade diamonds radiates behind everything; the invitation card itself carries high-contrast display caps (GATSBY over a wide-tracked GALA), fine rule ornaments with a diamond lozenge, a jade dashed bar, and a shimmering gold sweep that periodically crosses the paper. Below, a champagne coupe drawn in gold line holds a luminous pour: hovering (or pressing POUR, or clicking the glass) releases streams of rising golden bubbles. Pointer movement tilts the whole composition — skyline, sunburst and card rotate in opposing parallax like a stage set.
TECHNIQUES:
- Layered CSS 3D parallax: skyline, sunburst and card each carry a depth factor, transformed per-frame with lerped pointer coordinates (rotateY/rotateX on the card, opposing translate3d on the scenery layers)
- Geometry as ornament: inline-SVG stepped towers with window rectangles, a 16-ray sunburst with dashed and solid orbit rings, diamond lozenges in jade, and a rule-diamond-rule masthead divider
- The shimmer: a screen-blended diagonal white-gold gradient sweeping across the card on a 7.5s keyframe cycle
- Bubbles as six absolutely-positioned bordered spans with staggered animation-delays rising 64px from the coupe bowl, enabled on hover/focus or toggled persistently via the pour button
INTERACTION:
- Moving the pointer across the stage tilts the deco architecture — skyline drifts one way, sunburst the other, the card rotates gently in 3D; the scene relaxes on pointer leave
- Hovering or keyboard-focusing the coupe releases rising golden bubbles; clicking the glass or pressing POUR THE BUBBLES keeps the pour running (button becomes STOP THE POUR)
- All motion collapses gracefully under prefers-reduced-motion (static rays, bubbles held faintly lit, no tilt)
