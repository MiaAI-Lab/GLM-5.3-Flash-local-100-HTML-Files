I — Ground

Pulped mulberry, pressed thin as an apology. The paper is not a surface; it is a participant. Every fiber decides how far the black is allowed to travel.

II — Breath

Mist is the mountain's exhale. It moves slower than you, and it never quite conceals the ridge — it agrees with it, softens the argument between stone and sky.

III — Pressure

You have already pressed the page once, somewhere above. The bloom you left is still deciding its edges. Ink is patient; it keeps every promise it diffuses into.
