TITLE: STILL WATER
DESCRIPTION: A tranquil pond diorama rendered live on Canvas 2D. Four koi with bezier bodies and sine-tail swimming glide beneath bobbing lily pads with veined notched leaves and gradient blossoms; dragonflies with beating wings patrol the upper air; cattails sway at both banks; the sky reflects in the water and dimples with every click. A haiku line fades through the corner of the scene, a mono HUD tracks hour and population, and an hour slider dims the whole pond through mulberry dusk, moon glint and all. Scattering food sends the koi into excited flocking pursuit.
TECHNIQUES:
- Full-scene Canvas 2D: analytic hour-keyed palette interpolation (7 keys from dawn to night) driving water gradient, sky reflection band, shimmer streaks and caustic ribbons drawn with overlay compositing
- Koi as rotated bezier teardrops with clipped patch patterns (orange, white-orange, platinum, showa), quadratic tail fins swaying on a phase clock, and depth-based alpha
- Ripples as expanding stroked rings with attenuation; lily pads sense passing rings and bob via eased amplitude
- Crumb economy: food grains sink over time, koi steer with capped-turn pursuit of the nearest crumb, excited state multiplies speed and tail rate
- Dragonfly Lévy-ish wander with spring homing, wing-stroke stroked ellipses, and a soft shadow cast on the water below
INTERACTION:
- Click the water to raise ripples and drop three crumbs the koi can find
- "Scatter Food" seeds a cloud of crumbs that triggers urgent flocking pursuit; "Summer Rain" toggles falling drops that ring the surface; "Toss a Stone" makes a triple splash; "Verses" hides the rotating haiku
- Drag the Hour slider (05:00–22:00) to pass from clear afternoon into dusk vignette, moon glint and cold night water
PROMPT:
Design STILL WATER: a serene full-width pond canvas (aspect ~16:9) framed like a diorama under a soft serif header — an italic, lightly tracked title "Still Water", an eyebrow in letterspaced caps ("The Garden · Third Week of June · Late Afternoon"), and an italic intro paragraph. The water fills the canvas from a vertical gradient: sky-tinted at the horizon blending into deep green-blue (#3f6f5e → #204a3d), with a translucent sky reflection band in the upper half, drifting horizontal shimmer streaks, and overlay-composited caustic ribbons undulating slowly.

Populate it: seven lily pads (radial-gradient leaves with a notch wedge cut, radiating veins, gentle idle bob amplified when ripple rings pass), half bearing eight-petaled blossoms in pink #f2b5c4 or white; four koi of distinct varieties — orange, white-orange, platinum, showa (black patches) — each a bezier teardrop with clipped patch art, quadratic tail fins waving on individual phase clocks, pectoral fins, dot eyes and a white highlight, alpha-modulated by depth; three dragonflies hovering in the upper third with two stroked ellipse wings beating at ~42 Hz equivalent, teal bodies, and faint shadows on the water; swaying cattails (stems quadratic-curving in the breeze, brown seed heads, thin blades) along both edges; and drifting motes catching light.

Behavior: clicking anywhere spawns expanding ripple rings (fading strokes, width attenuating with radius) plus three food crumbs; pads bob when rings cross them. "Scatter Food" drops a 16-crumb cloud — all four koi switch to excited pursuit (2.5× speed, faster tails, capped turn rate toward nearest crumb, eating crumbs with small ripples). "Summer Rain" adds falling streaks that ring on impact. The hour slider re-keys the whole palette: at 19:00+ a mulberry #514663 vignette darkens the scene, a moon glint with halo appears, and the water cools toward night green.

Overlaid UI: a top-right mono HUD ("16:40 · clear / koi 4 · pads 6 · flies 3") on a translucent chip; a centered bottom haiku in italic serif that cross-fades between five verses with staggered line reveal ("still water — / the koi carry the sun / from pad to pad"). Below the pond, a two-panel control bank on dark glassy cards: "On the Bank" with four pill buttons (Scatter Food, Summer Rain, Verses, Toss a Stone), the Hour slider with a lily-green thumb on a dusk-gradient track and mono output, plus a naturalist's ledger line that updates narratively ("food on the water — four appetites become one parliament"); and "The Naturalist's Notes" with italic field-notes copy and a mono species line (Carassius auratus · Anax imperator · Nymphaea alba · Typha latifolia). Footer: spring-fed pond facts in italic with a mono signature. Teal selection color, green-on-dark scrollbar, dashed teal focus rings, reduced-motion shortens transitions and disables rain ripple spawn — the whole page should feel like sitting at the edge of real water in the last quiet hour of light.
