TITLE: Oscilloscope Music
DESCRIPTION: A playable oscilloscope in X-Y mode: pure sine oscillators feed channels A and B, and their ratio is drawn live as a glowing phosphor Lissajous figure on a green CRT with persistence trails, graticule, scanlines and a bright beam head. A weighted eight-key keyboard sets the root note (and starts the self-synthesized audio), nine interval presets (fifth, fourth, major third, tritone...) and a harmonic-series row reshape the knot in real time, while phase, detune, persistence, gain and auto-sweep knobs condition the beam. Amber overdrive warnings, engraved panel caps and mono readouts complete the instrument.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas phosphor simulation: translucent screen-green fills each frame so strokes decay with adjustable persistence, redrawn graticule, scanline and glass-glare overlays in CSS
- Lissajous math: x = sin(phi), y = sin(phi * ratio + phase), traced 64 segments per frame in "lighter" composite mode with a white-hot beam head
- WebAudio: two pure sine oscillators retuned with setTargetAtTime on every interval/phase/note change, channel gains tied to the gain knob
- Flicker-free engraved panel typography via dual text-shadow (dark top, light bottom) on caps labels; skeuomorphic screws, LEDs and drop-shadow keycaps
- Ratio naming table with cent offsets; click-on-screen sets the phase from the angle of the pointer
INTERACTION:
- Press A through K (or click the keys) to set the root note; the first press powers the audio
- Choose intervals or harmonics to morph the figure — unison circles, fifths knot, the tritone never closes
- Drag phase, detune, persistence, gain and auto-sweep knobs; click anywhere on the screen to throw the phase to that angle
- Hold figure freezes the trace; power toggles the drone; the beam warns OVERDRIVE in amber when gain runs hot
