TITLE: RAINY WINDOW
DESCRIPTION: A melancholic night scene framed as a lit window in a dark room. Behind the glass, a pre-rendered, CSS-blurred layer of amber and steel-blue bokeh city lights pulses over a skyline silhouette. On the glass itself, canvas raindrops crawl downward with wobbling paths, vertical refraction trails, elliptical lens heads with specular highlights — and they collide, merge, and accelerate when they touch. A translucent fog layer covers the pane; dragging wipes it away with a soft destination-out cloth stroke, and fog slowly re-condenses. Occasionally a multi-strobe lightning flash relights the whole room and over-brightens the bokeh. Muntin bars divide the panes; a faint interior reflection (plant, figure) sits in the corner; a quiet serif poem and mono timestamp sit below. Palette: night blues #0c1220/#1d2a44, amber bokeh #ffb26b, white-at-low-alpha rain highlights.
TECHNIQUES:
- Three stacked canvases: pre-rendered blurred bokeh (CSS filter blur(10px) + saturate), a fog layer driven by radial destination-out wipes, and a live rain layer with gradient trails and ellipse heads
- Drop physics: sinusoidal wobble drift, velocity growth on merge, area-conserving radius growth (r=√(r1²+r2²·0.8)), O(n²) merge pass capped at 90 drops on alternating frames
- Fog regeneration: 0.4%-alpha source-over veil per frame (throttled while wiping) so wiped paths heal gradually
- Lightning: randomized 9–21s scheduler firing a five-step opacity strobe sequence on a screen-blend flash layer plus a body class that over-brightens the bokeh filter
- Interior mood: gradient muntin bars with inset highlights, layered diagonal glass sheens with a blurred highlight lobe, faint SVG reflection silhouette, radial wall light-spill behind the frame
- WebAudio rain: looped pink-tinted noise through highpass→lowpass chain with a 0.05 Hz intensity LFO on the filter; gain ramps on toggle
INTERACTION:
- Drag across the glass to wipe the fog with a soft cloth stroke — the city blooms through where you pass; fog re-condenses over about a minute
- Watch drops collide, merge into fatter faster runners; trails lengthen with speed
- Click "Rain sound · Off" (or it becomes On) to start the synthesized rain — filtered noise that swells like weather; user-initiated only
- Press R to re-condense the fog instantly; a mono clock reads local time with a drifting "RAIN %" figure
- Reduced motion freezes fog regeneration but keeps the calm scene; everything pauses when hidden and cleans up on pagehide
