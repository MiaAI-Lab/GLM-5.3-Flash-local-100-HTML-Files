TITLE: T-MINUS
DESCRIPTION: A mission-control launch console wrapped around a fog-shrouded night pad. The flight canvas holds a red-tipped rocket with strobing tower beacon on a dark deck, smoke pooling at ignition, a camera that lifts with the vehicle through staged ascent into a star field that brightens with altitude. Console panels carry a hold-to-launch master switch with typed countdown callouts (RANGE GREEN, GUIDANCE INTERNAL, IGNITION SEQUENCE...), live telemetry (altitude, velocity, stage, throttle, fuel, Max-Q, apogee, MET), a green apogee plot drawn live, GO/NO-GO status lamps, and a timestamped flight log. Palette: console #101418, green #58d68d, amber #ffb454, alert #e05545, rocket white with #b3402a markings.
TECHNIQUES:
- Simplified vertical rocket physics: per-stage acceleration, exponential-atmosphere dynamic pressure for Max-Q, staged burn and separation flash, coast to apogee, camera track above 80 m
- Countdown theater: typed callouts at T-15 through T-1 with lamp and callout color escalation, rumble that rises through the final four seconds, and an abort window that hard-closes at T-2 (commit)
- Canvas night pad: sky that darkens toward space with altitude, twinkling star field with altitude parallax, strobing beacon, ground that scrolls away with the camera
- Synthesized WebAudio: brown-noise engine roar with gain following throttle, countdown clicks, separation thump, abort alarm; live apogee plot with peak marker on a second canvas
INTERACTION:
- Press and hold the red master switch (or Space) for 0.65s to arm — release early and the count needs a committed hand; the countdown runs with callouts and rising rumble; Abort stays armed until T-2, then the window closes
- During flight, Abort becomes RANGE SAFETY; the apogee plot traces altitude against mission time with an amber peak marker; fuel lamp goes amber then red as tanks drain
- After apogee the button becomes FLY AGAIN for an instant recount; Replay resets the console; flight log records every event with timestamps; footer tracks launches, successes, best apogee, aborts
