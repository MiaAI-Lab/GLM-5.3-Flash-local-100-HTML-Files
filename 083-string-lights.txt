TITLE: Fête
DESCRIPTION: An evening garden-party invitation beneath strings of festoon bulbs. Four catenary spans of warm-glowing bulbs sway between dark posts over a dusk-blue garden with drifting bokeh, wandering fireflies, foliage silhouettes and grass. A bulb-lit marquee spells FÊTE letter by letter on load, each glyph flanked by a strip of tiny bulbs. The invitation card flips in 3D to an RSVP back face. Signature moments: click any bulb to toggle its glow with a soft two-note chime, and a breeze slider sets how everything — bulbs and their halos — sways.
TECHNIQUES:
- Bulb strings computed along per-span catenary-like sag curves (parabolic droop from span width), each bulb an absolutely-positioned button with wire stub, glass, brass cap and a radial halo layer
- Sway engine: per-bulb phase, speed and tilt seed driven by sine functions; global breeze multiplier from a custom-styled range slider scales translation and rotation
- Lit-bulb glow via stacked box-shadows (tight bright core, 44px warm spread, 90px ambient) plus a separate 120px radial halo element that fades and scales in
- Canvas fireflies: 26 wandering agents with turning noise, vertical drift bounds and per-fly blink frequency, drawn as radial-gradient glow sprites
- Marquee reveal: staggered per-letter activation (opacity/translate/scale) lighting a 5-bulb strip and a text-shadow glow over each glyph
- 3D RSVP flip card with backface-visibility, gold-ruled ivory faces, and chosen-state RSVP buttons; CSS bokeh layer drifting on a 16s alternate loop
INTERACTION:
- Clicking a bulb toggles its warm glow and plays a soft synthesized chime (higher when lighting, lower when dimming); bulbs are real buttons with focus rings and aria-pressed state
- The BREEZE slider (0.00–1.00) live-scales the sway of every bulb — from dead calm to visible swinging strings
- TURN FOR RSVP flips the ivory card in 3D to reveal the reply face; choosing I WILL COME or WITH REGRET tints the chosen button green or oxblood, prints a handwritten-feel note, and chimes
- Fireflies drift continuously through the garden depth; the bokeh layer breathes behind everything; layout and bulb spans rebuild fluidly on resize
