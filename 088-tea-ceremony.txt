TITLE: Ichigo Ichie
DESCRIPTION: A wabi-sabi tea ceremony on tatami. A shoji-lit room with woven mats and a hanging kettle frames a clay chawan drawn on canvas; a tokonoma scroll with seasonal art (cherry blossoms or maple leaves) and a vertical motto hangs in the corner. Four step cards guide the ritual — warm the bowl, add the matcha, whisk, serve — and the whisk is yours: moving the pointer quickly back and forth over the bowl builds matcha froth particle by particle on a live froth meter, decaying if you pause. Serving tilts the bowl, sparkles a chime ring, and offers the tea. Steam curls from kettle and bowl throughout.
