TITLE: Ink Mountain
DESCRIPTION: A sumi-e ink-wash landscape on washi paper. Four graded mountain ridges — from a blurred ghost range to a near-black silhouette — sink at different rates as you scroll, crossed by drifting mist bands and a lone pine flicked from one brush gesture. On load, the horizon draws itself with a wet main stroke followed by two dry-brush echoes. A vertical poem column and a vermilion hanko seal stand on the right; four corner labels frame the page like a scroll mounting. Clicking anywhere drops an ink bloom that diffuses with a dark wet core, a soft halo and directional tendrils, like pigment finding the fibers of damp paper. Below the fold, a second-stanza essay section and a colophon with a miniature seal.
TECHNIQUES:
- Layered SVG mountain silhouettes filled with opacity-graded linear gradients and per-layer gaussian blur, transformed at data-depth-scaled rates for scroll parallax
- Washi paper texture built entirely from repeating-linear-gradient fibers, sparse radial-gradient flecks and a soft top-light vignette
- Self-drawing brush horizon via stroke-dasharray/dashoffset CSS animation — one thick wet stroke plus two delayed dry-brush passes
- Canvas ink blooms: radial-gradient halo, fading wet core, and randomized tendril blobs with individual lag factors and wobble, easing growth with cubic fade-out
- Drifting mist bands as blurred gradient strips with long alternating translateX animations plus scroll-linked margin offsets
- Vertical-rl poem column with text-orientation mixing, a rotated vermilion SVG hanko seal, and letter-spaced small-caps corner furniture
INTERACTION:
- Scrolling sinks the ridges at staggered depths while mist climbs more slowly, producing a slow exhalation parallax through the 230vh stage
- Moving the pointer applies an eased sideways "wind" to the whole ridge group
- Clicking or tapping anywhere spawns an ink bloom at that point — dark core first, then halo and tendrils drifting outward with wobble — capped at 14 simultaneous blooms; on reduced motion it becomes a single quiet stamped frame
- The hanko seal tilts and grows slightly on hover; focus outlines are vermilion; the layout reflows to a horizontal poem and shorter stage on phones
PROMPT:
