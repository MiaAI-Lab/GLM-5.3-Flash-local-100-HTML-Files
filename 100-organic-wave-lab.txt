TITLE: WAVE LAB
DESCRIPTION: An interference laboratory and the gallery's closing exhibit. A full-viewport pool renders up to five wave sources as living topography — the field is computed per 4px cell as a sum of outward-traveling decaying sine waves and painted as coarse-grid topography upscaled with smoothing, plus lighter-composited crest highlight lines. Three physics modes re-palette the entire page: Water (deep #0b2b3a pool, #7fd1c0 crests), Light (dark #14101f with violet→red spectral hue mapping), and Sound (warm #241a10 with oscilloscope amber #e8b45a). A serif lab plate carries the kicker "INTERFERENCE BENCH · CLOSING EXHIBIT" and live mono readouts (sources, λ, f, field); the console offers mode buttons, wavelength/frequency sliders, Place/Move/Clear tools, and a source-data list (phase and coordinates per source). The page opens with two sources already singing a standing wave. Signature moment: dragging two sources together collapses their rings into a beating moiré heartbeat — anti-nodes breathing in and out of existence, physics you can feel.
TECHNIQUES:
- Wave superposition: per-cell field = Σ sin((d/λ − t·f − φ)·2π) · 26/(26+d), normalized by source count, computed into a Float32Array and blitted via ImageData onto an offscreen canvas upscaled to viewport (CELL 4–5px, adaptive)
- Three palettes via body[data-lab] CSS variables feeding canvas colors; Light mode maps field value −1..1 through a violet→red HSL sweep; Water/Sound map |v|^1.5 between trough and crest RGB
- Crest texture pass: lighter-composite polylines through cells with v > 0.52 for churning surface detail
- Source visualization: pulsing emission rings (synced to t·f+φ), accent-colored cores with dark pin, mono S1–S5 labels; per-source phase in the data list
- Interaction model: Place tool taps new sources (max 5 with a barker toast), dragging any source retunes the field live, Move tool; two sources within 1.6λ triggers the "one heartbeat" toast
- Lab dressing: backdrop-blur panels, hairline borders, accent cross-fades over .8s, mono/serif typographic system, adaptive CELL on 4K, reduced-motion time dilation
INTERACTION:
- Tap empty water to place sources (up to five); drag any source to move it — the interference field reshapes in real time under your finger
- Drag two sources close together: their rings merge into beating moiré heartbeats and a toast marks the moment
- Slide λ (10–80px) and f (0.2–3Hz) to stretch and quicken the pattern; everything stays continuous
- Switch Water / Light / Sound physics — the whole page (pool, panels, accents) re-tints; keys 1–3 jump modes, C clears, M toggles Place/Move
- The source-data card lists every emitter's phase and coordinates; the pool starts pre-seeded with a two-source standing wave
