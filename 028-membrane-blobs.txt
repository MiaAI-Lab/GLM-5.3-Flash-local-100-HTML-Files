TITLE: CELL DIVISION
DESCRIPTION: A bioluminescent petri dish viewed down a dark-field microscope: a circular glass lens filled with translucent teal-and-blue membrane blobs, each carrying a pink nucleus that drifts inside its cell. An SVG gooey filter (Gaussian blur + contrast color matrix on the shared container) makes overlapping cells bridge with liquid necks — and when two cells overlap deeply enough, they genuinely merge back into one larger cell. Clicking any cell triggers a visible mitosis animation: the body elongates along a chosen axis, squeezes at its waist, and splits into two daughters that push apart. A four-gauge lab console tracks population, generation, biomass in square microns and colony age; on-lens readouts show magnification, mode, temperature, population, generation and mitosis count; a measurement reticle with rings, hairlines, corner brackets and a 50 µm scale bar completes the microscope staging.
TECHNIQUES:
- Gooey metaball rendering: one SVG filter on the world group — feGaussianBlur stdDeviation 7 into feColorMatrix (alpha 22/-11) — so cell circles fuse with organic necks wherever they come within overlap threshold
- Soft-body feel: per-cell brownian drift (cosine/sine noise forces), velocity damping, speed cap, dish-boundary reflection with restitution, and pairwise separation that permits slight overlap for gooey bridging
- Mitosis choreography: clicked cell locks a random division axis, then a 1.5 s rotate-a-stretch animation (translate→rotate→scale(1,stretch)→rotate back) elongates and pinches before a daughter cell spawns at the axis tip with opposing recoil velocities; generation bookkeeping follows
- Merge mechanics: deep overlap triggers mutual attraction over 0.8 s, then area-conserving radius (sqrt of summed areas, capped) at the weighted centroid — population drops, biomass persists
- Microscope staging: layered radial-gradient glass with inset shadows and vignette, CSS reticle (rings, crosshairs, corner brackets, scale bar), mono lab typography, gauge cards with colored top ticks, tabular numerals and aria-live narration
INTERACTION:
- Click any cell (hit radius 1.15r) to trigger mitosis and watch it divide into two daughters; the population, generation and mitosis counters tick up live
- Bring two cells together — drift will do it — and when they overlap past 55% of their separation threshold they merge with a liquid pull; the colony prefers company
- Drop Nutrient feeds the smallest cell a growth spurt and may trigger a spontaneous division; Drift On/Off freezes Brownian motion for close inspection; Re-culture reseeds eight fresh cells and resets the clock
- Watch colony age and a jittering temperature readout while the dish breathes at 60fps; every state change is narrated to screen readers
