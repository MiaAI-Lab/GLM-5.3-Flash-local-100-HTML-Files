TITLE: The Pigmentist
DESCRIPTION: A historical paint-mixing bench rendered as a working apothecary instrument. Twelve jars of genuine pigments — ultramarine, vermilion, yellow ochre, malachite, madder lake, smalt, Indian yellow, Prussian blue, raw sienna, lead white, carmine, verdigris — wait on the shelf with origin notes and mono codes. Pick two and they appear as stiff daubs on a glass slab; clicking the slab sweeps a brass-handled palette knife across the glass, smearing the two puddles through a trail of intermediate blends into a third color computed with subtractive absorbance math (so ochre and Prussian blue make a deep green, not gray). The ledger on aged paper reports the formula dots, the resulting hex, a poetic color name ("a cold viridian", "a funereal violet"), and both pigments' provenance. A double-click paints the result onto a swatch fan of paper leaves, each kept with its mix history.
TECHNIQUES:
- Subtractive-ish color mixing: sRGB converted to linear reflectance, absorbances (1 − R) weighted and summed like stacked glazes, with a granulation darkening term — yellow + blue yields green, complementary mixes go earthy
- Canvas glass slab: gradient glass with streak highlights, radial-gloss daubs drawn as layered bezier puddles with comb-ridge marks, and a 14-step animated smear of intermediate colors along the knife path
- Palette knife as inline canvas art: boxwood handle gradient, brass ferrule, curved steel blade; the knife re-enacts the sweep with a keyframed CSS transform on click
- Swatch fan: paper leaves fanned by rotation with glossy overlays, mono hex tags, click-to-recall provenance; leaves capped at 12
- Apothecary typography: letterspaced Palatine caps for headings, italic serif for notes and ledger lines, mono for codes and hex; aged-paper cards with ruled-line backgrounds and slight rotations on a warm bench wood ground
INTERACTION:
- Click jars to load one or two pigments on the slab (clicking again removes; picking a third swaps the pair)
- Click the slab and the knife sweeps — the two daubs smear into the blended third color with live formula and name readouts
- Double-click the slab to paint the current mix onto the swatch fan as a new leaf
- Click any leaf to recall its name, hex and provenance into the ledger; clear the fan with one press
- Full keyboard support on jars and leaves; hover/focus lift states; responsive from 360px to 4K
