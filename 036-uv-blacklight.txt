TITLE: BLACKLIGHT
DESCRIPTION: A fluorescent rave poster screaming silently inside a pitch-dark room. The poster — hand-lettered glow caps with chromatic misregistration, splatter drips, UV handprints, reactive stripes, rings and a barcode — exists only where your pointer's handheld blacklight beam passes, a violet-rimmed circle with drifting dust and a glinting tube. Hold to flood the beam wider; click to stamp permanent glow graffiti; flip All Lights and the full poster strobes over the whole page to a synthesized 124 BPM kick-hat-bass loop with a magenta room wash.
TECHNIQUES:
- Pre-rendered poster canvas revealed through per-frame masking: poster drawn to an offscreen canvas, composited with destination-in radial-gradient beam, then blitted over pure #060309 darkness
- Hand-lettering effect via layered canvas fillText: cyan/magenta ghost offsets under the main fill plus a destination-out inner bite for a printed misregistration look
- Procedural UV art: splatter blobs with drips, scaled handprints from ellipses, dashed concentric rings, gradient stripe tape, registration bursts, barcode strip and star field
- Beam physics: pointer smoothing, hold-to-widen radius with hysteresis, beam-relative dust motes, tube glint; synthesized rave kit (sine-drop kick, filtered-noise hats, saw-square bassline) locked to 124 BPM with beat-synced veil strobes
INTERACTION:
- Sweep the pointer (or finger) like a flashlight — only the beam's radius shows the poster; hold to widen the beam from SMALL to WIDE to FLOOD, release to shrink
- Click or tap to stamp permanent graffiti: splatter, glow tags (GLOW, ACID, RAVE...) or stencil stars, each with a soft blip and counter
- All Lights (L) strobes the whole poster to the beat with a flashing magenta veil and a pulsing BPM lamp; Sound (M) arms the synthesized loop; Repaint wall (C) erases stamps and re-prints the original poster
PROMPT:
