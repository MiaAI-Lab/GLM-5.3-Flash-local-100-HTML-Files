Programme Note

Mondrian arrived in New York and heard the city: not its buildings, but its traffic — lights stepping down avenues, taxis blinking at corners, jazz leaking from doorway to doorway. He answered with his only titled debt to music, Broadway Boogie Woogie, and replaced his black lattice with streets of living color.

This page lets that painting finish its thought. The cells are the piano's keys laid flat: press play and the reds walk the bass, the yellows answer on the off-beats, the blues hold the changes. Stop it, and the city holds its breath.
