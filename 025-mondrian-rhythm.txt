TITLE: BOOGIE WOOGIE
DESCRIPTION: A living tribute to Mondrian's Broadway Boogie Woogie: a hand-authored grid of white streets, black avenues and red/yellow/blue blocks that dances to an internal boogie-woogie rhythm. The page is an editorial split — a massive grotesque masthead ("BOOGIE WOOGIE" with a pulsing red square as the period) over a two-column body: the interactive painting on the left, and programme notes, an orchestration legend, and a hard-edged console on the right. Press Play and the composition swings at 132 BPM: red cells thump the walking bass on downbeats, yellow cells answer on off-beats, blue cells flash on chord changes, and every bar two colored blocks trade hues while a metronome stripe sweeps the console. Sound (optional, user-initiated) is a fully synthesized stride piano — sawtooth rolling bass C2-E2-G2-A2, triangle chord stabs, brushed noise hats — scheduled ahead of time against the audio clock. Clicking any cell cycles white→red→yellow→blue; "New Composition" re-deals the entire grid via random BSP subdivision with a staggered snapping animation.
TECHNIQUES:
- Composition as data: the opening grid is an authored 24×24 lattice rect list (streets, avenues, color blocks) rendered as absolutely-positioned buttons over a black plate; "New Composition" regenerates the lattice via binary space partitioning with guarded minimum cell sizes
- rAF choreography synced to musical time: per-cell roles (bass/answer/changes/rest) drive scale-and-hop transforms from the beat fraction; a bar-boundary hue-shuffle trades colors between two non-white cells each bar
- WebAudio stride piano: lookahead scheduler (25 ms interval, 120 ms horizon) against AudioContext time, swing eighths (62/38), sawtooth bass through a 620 Hz lowpass, triangle chord stabs per bar-position, highpassed noise-buffer hats, random pentatonic melody sparkles, all through a compressor
- Metro-brutalist UI: 3px black borders with hard offset shadows (box-shadow: 3px 3px 0), yellow hover states, red pressed states, a canvas-free CSS metronome stripe animated per bar, tabular mono metadata
- Accessibility and motion care: real <button> cells with aria-labels, polite live status line, spacebar/C/N keyboard shortcuts, reduced-motion mode that freezes pulsing and deal animations while keeping color logic
INTERACTION:
- Play/Pause (or spacebar) conducts the rhythm; the metronome stripe sweeps once per bar and the masthead status flips DORMANT/PLAYING
- Click any cell to cycle its color white→red→yellow→blue (each click plucks a color-mapped note when sound is on); press C to cycle every third cell at once
- "New Composition" (or N) re-deals a fresh asymmetric grid with cells snapping in one by one; the Tempo button steps through 96/112/132/152/176 BPM live
- Sound On starts the synthesized stride bassline only after an explicit click; muting keeps the visual rhythm running silently
