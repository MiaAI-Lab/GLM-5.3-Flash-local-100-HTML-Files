TITLE: THE CONTRAPTION
DESCRIPTION: A Rube Goldberg machine drawn as an inventor's bench sheet — pencil grid, italic serif title, red annotation arrows — on workshop paper. The contraption chains seven stages across the bench: a big red lever, a twelve-tile domino cascade, a brass marble on a gravity ramp, an enthusiastic desktop fan, a sail cart blown across the flat stretch, a pulley that trades falling weight for a released latch, and a cue ball dispatched through a sorting funnel into the toaster, which pops two perfectly imaginary slices over the breakfast plate. Hovering any part shows a hand-lettered tooltip with its role; pressing GO plays the whole chain with knock sounds and a running field log; Re-roll a stage randomizes one component (lever angle, domino count, marble size, fan blades, weight mass) and logs the calibration.
TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural SVG assembly: every stage built from createElementNS primitives (lever arm, domino rows, ramp, fan blades in a rotating hub group, sail cart, pulley wheel with rope path, zig-zag spring, funnel, toaster with brass slots, plate ellipses) plus dashed pencil "intention" paths and italic red stage numbers
- Promise-chained animation timeline: an rAF easing helper animates SVG attributes (path d for the lever, cx/cy for the marble and ball, transforms for cart/weight/latch, opacity for toast) so each stage completes before the next begins
- Overlay tooltip system: pointer position maps a data-part hit to a bordered paper tag with arrow, name and role, flipping side near the bench edge
- WebAudio knock percussion (triangle sweeps with exponential decay) fired per domino and impact; a stage re-roll system with five randomizers and a three-line field log
INTERACTION:
- GO runs the full chain reaction end to end (button disabled while running, stage status in the header, log lines accumulate in the footer); the toast banner announces breakfast
- Hovering parts explains their role with the annotated tag; the Re-roll a stage button randomizes one component and reports the new calibration
- Runs reset cleanly — lever, dominoes, marble, cart, weight, latch, ball and toast all return to their drawn positions before each run
