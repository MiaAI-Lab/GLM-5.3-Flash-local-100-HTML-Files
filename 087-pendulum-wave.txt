TITLE: Pendulum Wave
DESCRIPTION: Fifteen pendulums of descending length hang from a brass rail on a black stage and swing in a traveling wave — snake, ribbon, chaos — before snapping back into a single line every sixty seconds. Chalk trails trace each bob's path, a mono timer counts elapsed time against the 60-second grand cycle, and a "— UNISON —" note appears at the moment of re-alignment. Controls release the array from a perfect line, slow time to 0.15×, toggle the chalk trails and bob labels; any bob can be grabbed and displaced by hand, then eases back into the arithmetic.
TECHNIQUES:
- Pendulum wave math: bob i completes 51+i swings per minute, phase integrated as 2π/period × elapsed, each rendered as a rod + radial-gradient metallic bob over a brass top rail with hangers
- Chalk trails: an offscreen canvas accumulates per-frame bob dots and is faded with a translucent stage-colored fill, composited under the redrawn bobs
- Unison detection: average |sin(phase)| across all fifteen bobs below 0.02 triggers the re-unison note with a cooldown
- Pointer physics: grabbing a bob converts pointer angle to a clamped swing displacement (±1.15 rad) with a dashed grab ring; release decays the manual override back into the wave
- Brass fittings, apex-arc and floor chalk guides via dashed ellipses and lines; graded metallic bob palette from pale gold to deep bronze
- Reduced-motion fallback renders one composed snake-shaped still
INTERACTION:
- RELEASE FROM LINE zeroes the clock and phases so all fifteen start in a perfect row; the wave then unwinds into patterns and re-unites on the 60-second beat
- SLOW TIME toggles 0.15× playback for studying the mechanism (cycle timer rescales); CHALK TRAILS toggle the accumulating path layer; BOB LABELS shows hanging positions 1–15
- Grab any bob with the pointer to displace it — it holds your angle while held, then decays back into phase after release
- The unison note and cycle/elapsed readouts keep the beat legible; ladder chips list each bob's swings-per-minute
