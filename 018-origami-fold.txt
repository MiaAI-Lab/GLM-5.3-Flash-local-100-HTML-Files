TITLE: PAPER CRANE
DESCRIPTION: An origami study arranged as a paper studio workbench: a CSS 3D paper crane that assembles step-by-step through seven numbered folds, hanging from a vermilion thread inside a soft-lit stage. The model is built from clipped triangle-faced quadrant panels (warm kami paper #f6e7d8 front, #e8c9b0 back with visible fiber grain over the whole page), plus body sails, a keel, and a reverse-folded head that appear as folds progress. Beside the stage, a crease-diagram card swaps between seven hand-drawn SVG figures with dashed valley lines, solid mountain lines, and vermilion fold arrows, above an italic instruction panel. A numbered steps rail with circled serif numerals, prev/next controls, and arrow-key folding complete the workshop. Signature moment: each step folds the model with smooth 3D transitions (1.15s), and dragging the stage orbits the camera around the half-folded crane, which sways idly when you let go.
TECHNIQUES:
- CSS 3D origami: perspective orbiter wrapping a preserve-3d model of absolutely-positioned quadrant panels with clip-path triangle faces (front/back pairs with backface-visibility), transformed per fold-state class t2–t7 that accumulate
- Fold choreography via pure CSS: per-class transform matrices easing on a 1.15s cubic-bezier, sails/keel/head fading and swinging in at thresholds, dashed crease hairlines that vanish once folded
- Orbit camera: pointer-capture drag writing eased rotateX/rotateY custom properties with idle sine sway after 2.4s of rest
- Seven inline SVG crease diagrams with marker-end arrowheads, dashed valley / solid mountain legend, swapped by step with a fadeUp entrance
- Studio dressing: feTurbulence paper grain overlay, radial stage glow, hanging thread that swings only when the crane is complete, blurred floor shadow that widens as wings open
- Keyboard folding (←/→), aria-current step rail with done-state vermilion rings, reduced-motion fallbacks
INTERACTION:
- Click "Next fold" (or press →) to advance through The Square, Valley Folds, The Tent, Bird Base, Wings Spread, Neck &amp; Tail, The Head; ← steps back; any step in the rail jumps directly
- Drag anywhere on the stage to orbit the camera around the model in both axes; release and the crane settles, then breathes with a gentle idle sway
- Watch the diagrams, figure numbers (FIG. 1–7), and italic instructions update with each fold; completed steps earn vermilion ring numerals
- At fold seven the thread begins to swing — the crane is finished and hanging
- Reduced motion makes folds instant and disables sway; the loop pauses when the tab hides
