TITLE: WIND FIELDS
DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed generative flow-field canvas. Around 3,200 fine particles trace a layered trigonometric vector field across the whole viewport, leaving fading ink trails that accumulate like smoke on dark paper. Four curated palettes — Ember (#1a120b ground with #e2711d, #f2c14e, #8c3b2f inks), Moss (#0d1410 with #9bc53d, #e8fccd, #519e8a), Glacier (deep blues with #9fd3e8, #e8f6fb, #2e6f8e) and Dusk (violets with #b89ae0, #f2e8ff, #6a4b9e) — crossfade smoothly via keys 1–4 or a frosted palette bar of conic-gradient chips. A gallery-plaque title block in ui-serif sits bottom-left with a hatched hanging rail, material notes, and a mono coordinates readout top-left. The signature gesture: the mouse bends local wind vectors like a hand drawn through smoke, and clicking seeds a radial burst of fast bright particles.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas 2D flow field: angle from four summed sine/cosine octaves over scaled coordinates plus a radial ripple term, slowly evolving in time
- Additive 'lighter' compositing with per-frame translucent veil fills (source-over) for ink-trail accumulation and fade
- Palette crossfade by per-frame RGB lerp between the previous and current palette's ink colors
- Pointer-force field: velocity-based push plus a swirling tangential sine force with quadratic falloff inside a 190px radius
- Radial-gradient pointer glow, devicePixelRatio-aware canvas sizing, and particle recycling with area-based population cap (900–3,400)
- Gallery framing: multiply-blend vignette, hatched plaque rail, frosted backdrop-filter chips, mono tabular readouts
INTERACTION:
- Move the pointer to bend nearby wind vectors — particles swirl and stream around the cursor like smoke around a hand
- Click (or press-and-hold on touch) to seed a burst of 130 fast bright particles that rejoin the field
- Press keys 1–4 or click the conic-gradient chips to crossfade Ember, Moss, Glacier, and Dusk; a toast announces each palette
- Press H to slide the gallery plaque away for an unobstructed view
- The mono readout tracks live cursor coordinates and the field angle in degrees; reduced-motion pre-renders a calm still field
