TITLE: Hearth
DESCRIPTION: A hygge winter fireside rendered entirely on canvas in a virtual 1600x1000 room that letterboxes to any viewport. A stone hearth surround (individually shaded, seeded stones) frames a firebox where layered flame tongues burn in three depth passes — deep madder wash, orange mid tongues, bright golden cores with blue feet — over a glowing ember bed, with sparks rising, smoke drifting up the flue, and a whole-room warm glow that pulses with the fire's flicker. To the right, a window holds a moonlit night sky, distant rooftops with lit panes, and continuously falling snow; a candle and framed miniature and a hung stocking dress the mantel. On the tartan rug a black cat dozes, breathing, trailing floating z's — click it and it wakes, perks its ears, stretches a paw, and settles back down. A steaming mug sits on the hearth slab, and a wood basket waits to be clicked. A book-plate serif quote hangs at the top; a small-caps console below offers add-a-log, crackle ambience (self-synthesized WebAudio), a snow toggle, and wake-the-cat.
TECHNIQUES:
- Single canvas scene in virtual coordinates, scaled and letterboxed with setTransform so composition is identical from 360px to 4K
- Layered flame rendering: bezier flame-tongue blobs in three depth passes with lighter compositing, sine-stacked sway, and a distance-attenuated wind field that cursor gusts inject into
- Particle systems with caps: rising embers (90), window-bound snow (130), mug steam (26), chimney smoke (22), and floating z glyphs
- Pre-rendered static room layer (walls, paneling, floorboards, window, moon, ~60 seeded stones, mantel props, tartan rug clip-weave, wood basket) composited each frame for performance
- Whole-room radial glow and candle glow in lighter blend mode, plus a radial vignette; seeded mulberry32 PRNG so the stonework is stable
- WebAudio crackle: looped brown-noise rumble through a lowpass, randomly scheduled short bandpass noise-burst crackles scaled by fire intensity, plus whoosh, puff, and a purr (LFO-wobbled sawtooth) — all created only after a user click
- Cat drawn parametrically with eased wake/stretch/settle timeline states; fuel meter of CSS log slots; full prefers-reduced-motion static frame
INTERACTION:
- Wave the cursor through the firebox and air puffs bend the flames and push embers; clicking inside the firebox sends a stronger puff and a small flare
- "Add a log" (or clicking the wood basket) stacks another log in the fire, spikes the intensity, throws a burst of embers, plays a whoosh-and-swell, and refills the fuel meter — five logs maximum, burning down slowly over time
- "Crackle ambience" toggles the self-synthesized fireplace soundscape (user-initiated only, suspends on toggle-off)
- "Let it snow" pauses and resumes the window snowfall
- Click the cat (or press "Wake the cat") to see it wake, stretch, and resettle; hover states and focus rings on every control; the scene pauses when the tab hides
PROMPT:
Create a single self-contained HTML page titled "HEARTH — A Winter Fireside": a dark, warm hygge scene, 100% canvas-drawn, no images. Palette: room #221c18 and deeper #161210, stone #6b6560 and #4a4643, fire madder #c1440e, ember orange #ff9a3c, flame gold #ffd166, snow #dfe9f5, cream text #efe3cd, tartan reds #7e2d26 and greens #2e4436. Typography: an italic book-plate serif (Iowan Old Style/Georgia) for the quote, spaced small-caps system-ui for all controls.

Layout: full-viewport canvas. Upper right, a thick-framed window: night-sky gradient, glowing moon with halo, silhouette rooftops with two lit golden panes, cross mullions, and snow steadily falling inside the glass, settling line at the sill. Above the scene, an HTML book-plate: hairline rules around the italic quote "The fire says nothing, and yet the whole room leans in to listen." and a small-caps citation "FROM THE ALMANAC OF LONG WINTERS · PLATE THE THIRD". Below, a small-caps console: a stoker line with five log-slot pips and the note "the fire holds one log", then four bordered buttons — ADD A LOG, CRACKLE AMBIENCE, LET IT SNOW, WAKE THE CAT — plus a faint hint line about waving the cursor through the flames.

Draw the fire as three tongue layers (deep madder wash, five orange mid tongues, three golden cores, tiny blue feet) using quadratic-curve flame blobs whose sway reads from a wind field: a base breeze of stacked sines plus decaying radial gusts injected by cursor movement (rate-limited) and clicks. Above the logs, an additive ember bed gradient; embers rise, flicker, and fade with a 90-particle cap; faint smoke climbs past the mantel. A large additive radial glow centered on the firebox breathes with a two-sine flicker and lights the whole room; the candle gets its own tiny flame and halo. Logs are drawn as shaded round-rect cylinders — one per unit of fuel, stacked with alternating tilt — and fuel slowly burns down from 5 to a baseline over minutes.

The cat breathes (sine-scaled body), flicks its tail slowly, and emits occasional floating italic z glyphs. Clicking it runs a state machine — waking (head lifts, ears perk over 0.6s), stretch (body elongates, a front paw extends, tail flicks fast for 1.6s), settle (eases back over 0.9s) — with golden eyes open while awake, then back to sleep.

Audio: on the first click of CRACKLE AMBIENCE, create an AudioContext; play a looped lowpass brown-noise rumble whose gain tracks fire intensity, and schedule short bandpass noise-burst crackles at random intervals that speed up as the fire grows. Adding a log triggers a band-swept whoosh plus a quick swell of crackles; clicking the fire plays a soft puff; waking the cat plays a low LFO-wobbled purr. Nothing autoplays; toggling off suspends the context.

Engineering: one style block, one script block, zero network requests, system fonts, custom selection color, virtual 1600x1000 scene scaled to fit any viewport with letterboxing, devicePixelRatio capped at 2, one requestAnimationFrame loop paused on tab-hide, static single-frame render under prefers-reduced-motion, and pointer events used throughout so it works on touch.
