TITLE: Gradient Atelier
DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed interactive mesh-gradient laboratory set on near-black glass chrome. Five to nine draggable color "pins" blend into a silk-smooth gradient rendered on a low-resolution offscreen canvas and upscaled for effortless softness. A frosted control card cycles four curated pigment recipes (Sunset, Ocean, Orchid, Matcha), a serif wordmark anchors the top-left, and a mono plaque at the foot displays the current recipe as raw hex values. Idle sine drift keeps the cloth breathing; double-tapping sows or culls pigments with an elastic snap; a film-grain overlay and radial vignette give the whole plate a printed, gallery-wall finish.
TECHNIQUES:
- Mesh blending via radial gradients painted at 1/6 resolution onto an offscreen canvas, upscaled with high-quality image smoothing
- Per-node sine oscillators (phase, speed, amplitude) driving perpetual idle drift in fractional coordinates
- Frame-rate-independent RGB tweening (exponential decay) for seamless palette crossfades on nodes and base
- easeOutElastic spawn and easeInBack squash-and-snap cull animations; pulse swells triggered from swatch chips
- Frosted glassmorphism with backdrop-filter, custom film-grain tile stamped once to a pattern-filled overlay canvas, radial vignette, drawn pin markers with influence rings and hex labels, pointer-capture dragging, unified double-tap detection for mouse and touch, tiny user-initiated WebAudio pluck/chord engine
INTERACTION:
- Drag any pin to steer the blend (drift pauses on the grabbed node so the grab never pops); double-tap empty cloth to sow a new pigment, double-tap a pin to cull it (minimum three)
- Click swatches or plaque hex chips to "taste" a pigment — matching nodes swell; arrow buttons or N/P/1-4 cycle recipes; D toggles drift, G toggles grain, M toggles sound
- Hovering a pin reveals its live hex label, influence ring and crosshair ticks; every action confirms with a glass toast
