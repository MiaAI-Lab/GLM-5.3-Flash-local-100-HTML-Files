TITLE: KALEIDO
DESCRIPTION: A Victorian optical toy rebuilt as a living drawing instrument: a deep-velvet page holding an ornate brass lens in which everything the visitor draws is folded into kaleidoscopic bloom. The lens is a conic-gradient brass bezel with a knurled repeating-conic edge, curved engraved text ("THE MIRROR LANTERN KALEIDOSCOPE"), a crosshair reticle on hover, a film-grain overlay and a glass specular sheen. Strokes are tapered, speed-weighted ribbons with a soft glow pass, replicated through 3 to 12 mirror wedges (with optional true reflection symmetry for the full 2N folds). Four pigment palettes — Jewel, Pastel, Ember, Ocean — recolor the five brush swatches instantly; a Replay action re-lays every recorded stroke through the current mirrors like time-lapse bloom, and changing the mirror count morphs all existing art in place. A brass title plate beneath reads "KALEIDO · A drawing instrument of mirrored light"; mono census lines track mirrors, palette and strokes laid.
TECHNIQUES:
- Dual-layer canvas lens: a committed "art" canvas plus a live-preview canvas cleared and repainted at ~80 fps during drawing, both DPR-aware and circularly clipped by the stage
- Kaleidoscope replication: each polyline is redrawn under rotate(base) and mirrored scale(1,-1) transforms per wedge, toggling between rotational-only and true mirror symmetry — so changing the mirror count instantly re-folds every recorded stroke from normalized local coordinates
- Tapered speed-weighted ribbon rendering: per-segment widths from a sine envelope multiplied by a speed factor (slow = lush ribbon, fast = comet), alpha eased by speed, plus a wide low-alpha glow pass; reduced-motion falls back to single flat strokes
- Brass optics in pure CSS: conic-gradient bezel with repeating-conic knurl mask, layered inset shadows, engraved SVG textPaths on circular arcs, radial glass highlights, and an overlay film grain
- Progressive replay engine: strokes are stored as normalized local polylines and re-committed one by one on a timer scaled to stroke count, with polite live-region narration; pointer capture for clean mouse/touch/pen drawing
INTERACTION:
- Draw with mouse, finger or pen anywhere in the lens; strokes appear mirrored in real time on the live layer, then commit with a glow on release
- Step the mirror count 3-12 with the brass stepper (or +/-) and watch the entire existing drawing re-fold to the new symmetry instantly; the engraved caption updates ("N MIRRORS MAKE 2N FOLDS OF LIGHT")
- Switch between Jewel, Pastel, Ember and Ocean palettes (pressed-state pills with color chips) and pick one of five recolored brush swatches; the size slider scales the ribbon
- Replay blooms the whole drawing back stroke by stroke; Clear wipes the lens; Symmetry Toggle flips between mirrored and rotational folding — all narrated through an aria-live status
