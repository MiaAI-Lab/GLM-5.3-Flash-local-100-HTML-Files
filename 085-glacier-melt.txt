TITLE: Ice Memory
DESCRIPTION: A climate data story told in one draggable century. A procedurally drawn glacier valley — far ridges, scree-textured rock walls, a crevassed ice tongue with transverse blue bands — shrinks as a year slider (1900–2024) sweeps forward, while a milky proglacial lake grows from the snout and eventually swallows the valley where the ice stood. Painted marker rocks record where the tongue reached in 1936, 1966, 1988, 2004 and 2016, turning vermilion as the tongue retreats past them. Live readouts track ice extent, tongue length, lake area and temperature anomaly; three sparkline cards (temperature, mass balance, lake growth) draw themselves up to the chosen year with dashed futures ahead; and era captions fade between decades of quiet narrative.
TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural valley: a bedrock height profile with mid-valley rock step and seeded jitter, two valley-wall polygons with scree stroke texture, and layered parallax ridges
- Glaciology-as-math: melt fraction driven by a smoothed warming-anomaly curve (with mid-century plateau), tongue length and thickness derived from it, crevasse band density scaling with melt, and lake level rising on the lowest bed point of the span
- Sky and palette warm continuously with the anomaly via a hex-mix interpolation function — the whole scene's mood shifts from ice-blue to pale amber
- Canvas sparklines with area fill up to the present year, dashed "future" segment, baseline rule, and a present-day dot; re-rendered per scrub position
- Gradient-styled range scrubber whose track maps ice-blue to coral across the century; decade tick row; era captions cross-fade on decade change
- Somber editorial furniture: 2px ink borders with offset shadows, mono data readouts, serif narrative columns, small roman year stamps on canvas rocks
INTERACTION:
- Drag the year slider (or ArrowUp/Down for 5-year jumps) and the valley redraws instantly: ice retreats, lake rises, sky warms, markers recolor, readouts and sparklines advance — a century in seconds
- Slow scrubbing fades in new era captions — seven passages of narrative from hay-field years to school trips and a thin white comb in a high notch
- All four readout cards and three sparkline cards update in lockstep; sparklines show the record to date plus a dashed ghost of what comes after
- Fully keyboard-reachable slider with aria labels; reduced motion removes caption cross-fades (text swaps instantly)
