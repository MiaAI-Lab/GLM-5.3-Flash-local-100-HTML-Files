TITLE: PIXELOPOLIS
DESCRIPTION: An isometric miniature city rendered on a single canvas: a 16×16 diamond-grid island of chunky buildings in four color wards (gold #f2d16b civic, terra #e8875f makers, moss #9bd0a0 gardens, slate #8fa3b8 sleepers), gridded roads with dashed centerlines, park trees, street lamps, tiny looped vehicles with night headlights, and drifting clouds that cast moving ground shadows. A full day-night cycle relights thousands of individually-tracked tiny windows (lit fraction follows the hour, with a few flickering), while the sky gradient, sun/moon arc, and stars follow. The HUD is a chunky panel system with mono stats (population, blocks, cars, lit windows, hour, focused block). Signature moment: a time-of-day slider scrubs the city from midnight through neon dusk in one drag — dusk warms every surface amber and the windows surge alight — and clicking any building cycles its lights (auto → forced on → forced off) with a happy bump animation.
TECHNIQUES:
- Isometric projection: gx/gy → screen via 2:1 diamond math with painter's-algorithm draw list (tiles, then trees/buildings/cars/lamps sorted by gx+gy), DPR-aware resize and scale-to-fit island
- Day-night engine: keyframed sky gradients, daylight() curve, duskWarmth() amber tint mixed into every surface color, nightF for stars/lamps/headlights, and a litFraction() curve driving per-window probabilities
- Seeded procedural generation (mulberry32): deterministic blocks, window grids (per floor × per face × 3), trees, cars, clouds, stars
- Window simulation: each window has an index threshold compared against the hour's litFraction plus occasional sine flicker; forced-on/off overrides per clicked building; bump spring on click
- Chunky HUD: offset box-shadow panels, border-styled range slider with day→night gradient track and --pct custom property, play/pause cycle button with inline SVG icon swap
- Mouse picking: point-in-diamond and bbox tests against cached building polygons; focus readout and pointer cursor feedback
INTERACTION:
- Drag the time-of-day slider (or press ←/→) to scrub the city through dawn, noon, dusk, and night; the mono clock names each period
- Click the round play button (or Space) to let the hours roll automatically; press again to hold the moment
- Hover any block to see its census line (block number, floors, ward); click to cycle its lights with a little bounce
- Cars loop their routes continuously with headlights after dark; clouds drift and shadow the streets; stars twinkle at night
- Reduced motion freezes cars/clouds but keeps the scrub interactive; the loop pauses when hidden
