Observations continuous

19 May, evening. The meadow keeps its own ledger. The catchfly opens as the light fails — five petals, a small pale lamp. The columbine will not open for me at all; it waits, I think, for a moth I have never met. The poppy spends itself in a single morning and calls the expense reasonable.
20 May, rain. Pressed all three between the pages of this book. Under the loupe the hairs on the stem stand like a forest; a whole weather of their own. What the eye reads as green is a parliament of greens — see the swatches, mixed from the crushed leaf and the crushed honesty of naming them.
