TITLE: Bonsai Studio
DESCRIPTION: A procedural bonsai atelier staged in a tokonoma alcove. A recursive, tapering-branch tree grows from a shallow glazed ceramic pot with moss and gravel, its silhouette reshaped by three style presets (Chokkan, Moyogi, Kengai), each with its own glaze color and keeper's verse. A vertical serif title column with a carved red seal anchors the left; moss-toned panels on the right hold the poem, style chips, season light buttons and keeper's tools. Seasonal light washes the alcove from spring to winter while leaves drift through the frame. Dragging with shears snips branches with an audio snap; Grow season extends every living tip.
TECHNIQUES:
- Recursive limb generation with tapering multi-segment bark strokes, bark highlights, trunk-chain bending per style, and clustered ellipse foliage
- Canvas alcove scene: gradient backdrop, radial seasonal light, wall grain, hanging scroll suggestion, glazed pot with sheen, feet, gravel and moss pads, plus a soft wall shadow pass of the whole tree
- Breeze sway via per-branch sine wobble scaled by depth; falling-leaf canvas layer with season-dependent spawn rate; soft-light season wash overlay
- Pruning by point-to-segment distance with recursive subtree culling and leaf filtering; grow-season tip extension; fully synthesized WebAudio shears (highpass noise burst + falling square chirp) and foliage rustle
INTERACTION:
- Toggle Shears (S) and drag across any branch to snip it — subtrees and their leaves fall away with a snap; R restores every cut; New seedling regrows a differently inclined tree
- Grow season (G) extends each living tip with new branches and foliage, aging the tree one year per season
- Style chips (keys 1-3) reshape the entire tree and swap the pot glaze, caption, and verse; season buttons re-light the alcove; Breeze toggles the sway; every interaction confirms with a toast

Layout: a tokonoma frame (timber gradient, inset lintel shadow) containing a three-column alcove — left, a vertical-rl serif title column reading two kanji with a mono sub-line and a rotated red seal at the bottom; center, a canvas scene of the bonsai; right (hidden under 920px), moss-toned panels: the keeper's poem in serif, style chips (Chokkan formal upright / Moyogi informal upright / Kengai cascade, pressed state in ink black), four season buttons that tint the scene via soft-light wash, and tool buttons (Grow season, Shears, Breeze, New seedling).
