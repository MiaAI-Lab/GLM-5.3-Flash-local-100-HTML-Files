TITLE: Sky Lanterns
DESCRIPTION: A dawn balloon festival rendered on Canvas 2D: a violet-to-peach gradient sunrise with a pulsing sun and drifting clouds, three layers of parallax violet mountains that slide with the wind, a green launch field with mown stripes and striped crew tents, and a fleet of nine procedurally patterned hot-air balloons (bands, gores, chevron, harlequin) with woven baskets, burner flames and interior glow. A tilted crew checklist card tracks launch tasks, a canopy-pattern picker dresses the hero balloon, a wind slider re-drifts the whole fleet, and firing the burner lifts a balloon with a flame whoosh. Poster serif masthead and mono crew annotations tie it together.
TECHNIQUES:
- Three-layer sine-generated mountain ridges drawn twice (wrapped) for seamless wind-driven parallax; valley haze gradient between layers
- Procedural balloon envelopes via cubic bezier outlines with clip-masked pattern fills (horizontal bands, vertical gores, chevron rows, harlequin diamonds), seam strokes, and directional sun shading
- Buoyancy physics: exponential rise impulses from burner firings decaying back to an anchor altitude, per-balloon sway/bob phase, baskets swinging on a rope pivot
- Interior glow and flame rendered with radial gradients in "lighter" composite; flame height jitters at 24 Hz
- Crew checklist with animated CSS checkmarks, HOLD state when wind exceeds 6 knots; medal medallion with slow-rotating dashed ring
- WebAudio: looped filtered-noise wind bed whose volume follows the slider, plus shaped noise-buffer whooshes and a sine ding, all user-initiated
INTERACTION:
- Click any balloon (or press B) to fire its burner — flame roars, glow floods the envelope, and the balloon rises with a whoosh
- Drag the wind slider (0–10 kt) to change fleet drift speed, sway amplitude, cloud motion, ridge parallax and the audio wind bed; over 6 kt the checklist flags HOLD
- Pick a canopy pattern for the hero balloon; each choice ticks a checklist item
- Sound toggle enables the self-synthesized dawn ambience
