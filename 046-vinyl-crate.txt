TITLE: CRATE DIGGING
DESCRIPTION: A late-night record shop listening station. In a wooden crate fanned with six generative album sleeves — kraft covers drawn as inline SVG art (concentric rings, cobalt blocks, pink arcs, silo stripes, green hiss blobs, copper fans) — the browser browses, pulls a sleeve, and loads it onto a Rexotone MK-3 deck. The felt platter spins the disc, the tonearm swings over with a thump, handmade vinyl crackle starts popping, and each record performs its own synthesized jazz loop: ballad, modal vamp, waltz, twelve-bar blues, lo-fi lullaby and uptempo swing, complete with walking bass, comping stabs, melody and brushes. Flipping the sleeve reveals serif liner notes with a tracklist and a rubber stamp.
TECHNIQUES:
- Generative inline-SVG cover art per album on kraft card with printed keyline borders
- Vinyl simulation: repeating-radial-gradient grooves, conic-gradient light sheen, per-album label gradient via CSS custom properties, CSS rotation loop gated by animation-play-state
- Tonearm built from divs (pivot, rod, headshell) rotating on transform-origin with cubic-bezier easing; needle-drop timed to the audio thump
- WebAudio jazz engine: lookahead bar scheduler, chord-tone comping with swing-delayed offbeats, random-walk melody over mode scales, walking bass, high-passed noise brushes, looped hand-averaged crackle buffer plus random pop charm, master compressor
- 3D sleeve flip with backface-hidden faces; marquee now-playing strip with duplicated lane content
INTERACTION:
- Hover the crate to browse the fan; click a sleeve to pull it, load the platter and see its gold dot
- "Needle Drop" swings the arm — after the drop you hear crackle, thump, then the record's own progression; press again (or Space) to lift the needle
- Click the big sleeve (or "Flip Sleeve", or Enter) to read liner notes; volume slider feeds the master gain live
