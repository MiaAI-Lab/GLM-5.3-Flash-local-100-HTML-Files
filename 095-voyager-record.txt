TITLE: THE GOLDEN RECORD
DESCRIPTION: A tribute to Voyager, rendered as a deep-space listening room. A procedurally etched golden disc (#d4af37 with #f3e5ab highlights, ~24 concentric groove rings, engraved data arcs, a pulsar-map hint, and a cream label reading "Sounds of Earth — Voyager · 1977") sits on an invisible turntable with a chrome tonearm that swings in when the needle drops. Nine "Sounds of Earth" movements are synthesized live in WebAudio — thunder with crackle bursts, bird chirps, cricket ticks, ocean surf swells, fire, an 18-beat human heartbeat (lub-dub at 55/min), twelve tonal greeting motifs, a humpback whale solo, and alpha-drifting brainwaves — each selectable, auto-advancing, with a playing ring and animated EQ bars. Mono NASA-style telemetry counts Voyager 1's distance (~25 billion km) ticking at ~17 km/s in real time alongside MET and light-delay hours, over a drifting twinkling starfield. Type: mono telemetry, serif dedications. The signature moment: dropping the needle — the arm swings, the disc turns, Earth's sounds play, and the distance counter keeps ticking relentlessly beneath it all.
TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural SVG grooves: alternating dark/light concentric circles (r 108–186 step 3.4), five engraved data arcs computed from polar coordinates, dashed rim, and pulsar rays, over a CSS golden radial-gradient disc with conic sheen sweep and inner shadows
- WebAudio sound design per movement: exponential rumble sweeps + randomized triangle crackle for thunder; 13 randomized triangle chirps for birdsong; 46×3 square ticks at 4.3kHz for crickets; looped pink-noise through lowpass with 4 gain swells for surf; noise bed + crackle density for fire; scheduled sine lub-dub pairs for heartbeat; 12 four-note sine motifs with a filtered sawtooth "voice" finale for greetings; 4 long frequency-gliding whistles for the whale; 9.4Hz→6.2Hz LFO amplitude-modulating a 218Hz sine for brainwaves
- Auto-advancing playlist with per-track durations returned by each performer, stopVoice() master-recreation for hard cuts, and EQ bar animation on the active row
- Live telemetry: distance = elapsed-since-launch × 16.9995 km/s + calibration offset, updated every second with light-delay computed from c
- Canvas starfield with per-star twinkle phase, lateral drift, and a faint gold solar glare; CSS tonearm rotation on .playing
- Serif dedication quote block and mono credit lines; gold selection and focus rings
INTERACTION:
- "Drop the needle" swings the tonearm, starts the spin (33⅓-feel ~17°/s), and begins the thunder — press again to lift the needle and return to silence
- Click any of the nine movements to cue it directly; tracks auto-advance in sequence while playing; the active row glows with "· NOW ETCHED" and dancing EQ bars
- Watch the telemetry: distance in km, mission elapsed time, signal delay in light-hours — ticking even while you listen
- The starfield drifts and twinkles behind everything; reduced motion stills the disc and EQ but keeps the audio and counter alive
