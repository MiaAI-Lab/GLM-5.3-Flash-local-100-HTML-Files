TITLE: Zen Grid
DESCRIPTION: Sudoku reframed as a garden meditation: a wooden board of tatami-toned tiles sits in a raked-gravel room with moss accents, wrapped in layered morning mist that thins and clears as completed rows lift it, while a breathing circle paces an eight-second in/hold/out cycle. The generator carves a fresh fair puzzle (unique-solution verified by a counting backtracker) every time; digits are placed as engraved serif stones with koto-style pentatonic plucks, pencil marks tidy themselves, mistakes shake in vermilion, and finishing the grid releases a drift of cherry blossoms with the words "The garden is at peace."
TECHNIQUES:
- Fair puzzle generation: randomized solved grid via backtracking, then clue-carving with a two-solution-count uniqueness check on every removal (MRV heuristic)
- Pure DOM board with highlight logic (row/column/box peers, same-digit, selection moss ring), CSS engraving via dual text-shadows on given digits
- Mist atmosphere from three stacked blurred radial-gradient layers whose opacity is bound to rows-cleared progress; mix-blend screen for luminous fog
- Breathing timer as pure CSS keyframed scale cycle with cross-fading phase captions (breathe in / hold / out / rest)
- rAF cherry-blossom particle system with sine sway, rotation, cap of 90 petals and cleanup on exit; vermilion error shake keyframes
- WebAudio koto pluck: triangle fundamental + detuned sine partial through a closing lowpass; bellRow fanfare with inharmonic partials; soft sine thud for mistakes
INTERACTION:
- Click a cell (or arrow-key to it) and place digits 1-9 via the wooden stone pad, keyboard, or number keys; erase with 0/Backspace
- Toggle pencil marks (P) to pencil candidate digits into empty cells; placing a stone sweeps that digit from peer marks
- Hint (H) fills the selected or first empty correct digit, three per puzzle; "Look again" shakes every wrong placement in vermilion
- Breathe with the circle; each placement counts a breath in the ledger; finished rows ring a bell and lift mist
- "New garden" (N) regenerates a fresh fair grid; Koto toggle enables the self-synthesized soundscape
