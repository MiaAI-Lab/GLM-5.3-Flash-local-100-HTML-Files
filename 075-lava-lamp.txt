TITLE: Mood Lounge
DESCRIPTION: A 1970s parlor assembled around one glowing lava lamp. The room is built in CSS — deep plum walls, wood plank floor, a plush shag rug flecked with colored pile, a sunburst brass clock with genuinely ticking hands, and stacked vinyl — while the lamp itself is a true metaball simulation on canvas: wax blobs warm at the bulb, rise, cool at the crown, and fold back down, rendered on a low-res scalar field that is thresholded, smoothed and upscaled for liquid-soft edges. The lamp's glow washes the whole room through crossfading screen-blend layers that follow the lamp's position; turning the heat knob speeds the blobs, brightens the filament and shifts the ambience from drowsy to simmering. Three colorways (sunset, grape, avocado) re-tint wax, liquid, glow and chrome; tapping the glass jolts the wax; an optional ambient purr hums beneath it all. Rounded heavy display type in chrome gradient, a groovy italic subtitle, and mono console labels complete the lounge.
TECHNIQUES:
- Metaball lava: per-pixel inverse-square field sum over moving blobs plus a bottom reservoir and top meniscus pool, thresholded with smoothstep alpha and a hotter core highlight, computed on a half-resolution ImageData and drawn upscaled with smoothing
- Thermal physics per blob: heat gain near the bulb, cooling near the crown, buoyancy proportional to temperature and heat setting, sine wander, soft container bounds following the vessel's tapered profile
- Room glow via two stacked radial-gradient overlays crossfading on colorway change, mix-blend-mode screen, positioned at the lamp's live viewport anchor through CSS custom properties
- Pure-CSS scenography: conic-gradient sunburst clock with JS-rotated hands, repeating-radial vinyl grooves, multi-layer radial-gradient shag pile, clip-path chrome cap and base with brushed-metal stripes
- Chrome display type via background-clip:text gradient with drop-shadow; entire palette (wax, liquid, glow, accent) swaps through CSS custom properties on colorway change
- WebAudio ambience: detuned sine drone through a lowpass whose gain and cutoff follow the heat knob, plus randomly scheduled sine bubble blips; user-initiated toggle, suspend on stop
INTERACTION:
- Drag the heat knob (or arrow keys; double-click resets) from cold to simmer: blob tempo, buoyancy, bulb brightness, room glow strength, drone volume and filter all track the setting, and the readout reports heat percent and filament temperature
- Choose a colorway swatch — sunset, grape neon, or avocado — and the wax, liquid, lamp halo, room glow and accent type all crossfade to the new mood
- Tap the glass: the wax shudders, blobs jolt and warm, the bulb flashes, and (if ambience is on) a bubble blip plays
- Toggle the ambient purr for a warm synth drone with irregular bubbles; it never autoplays and suspends on toggle-off
- The clock tells real time; the scene pauses on tab-hide; reduced motion renders a calm still lamp with all controls still live
