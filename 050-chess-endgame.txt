TITLE: ENDGAME
DESCRIPTION: A contemplative chess study in a lamplit room. A warm wooden board (honey #e8d3a9 and walnut #9a6b44 squares in a brass-outlined frame) hosts three carved pieces — ivory king and queen against a lone walnut king — drawn as inline SVG with gradient fills and drop shadows. Click or drag to move; legal squares show carved dots, the last move glows amber, the defending king pulses crimson in check. A notation ledger fills in figurine algebraic (♕d1–d5+, ♚e8–f7) beside twin mono clocks, a study-engine AI defends honestly, and checkmate triggers a slow spotlight sweeping the board while the ledger records 1–0 and the study is pronounced complete.
TECHNIQUES:
- Full K+Q vs K legality engine: ray/adjacency attack maps, pseudo-move generation filtered by simulated king safety, mate and stalemate detection
- Carved-feel pieces: hand-drawn inline SVG (crossed-cross king, five-point coronet queen) with per-piece linearGradient fills, ink strokes and drop-shadow filters
- Piece movement as absolutely positioned divs with left/top percentage transitions; drag support with pointer capture, live ghost translation, and hover-target square highlighting
- Checkmate spotlight: a screen-blended radial glow sweeping the board on an infinite alternate keyframe loop, plus a delayed banner and a three-note chime
- Study-room atmosphere: lamp-pool radial gradients, ruled-paper ledger texture, brass hairlines, small-caps serif annotations
INTERACTION:
- Click a white piece then its target (dots mark legal squares), or drag the piece directly; Escape deselects, U undoes a full move
- The engine replies after a thoughtful pause; the ledger writes each half-move with +/# suffixes and a final 1–0 or ½–½ result line
- New Study resets, Undo rewinds both plies, Sounds toggles the wooden tock and mate chime; clocks tick for whichever side is thinking
