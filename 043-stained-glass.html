Rose Window

The master glazier set twelve hundred pieces of glass into stone, so that the faithful might read the light the way they read the psalter — one jewel at a time. Drag the sun across the sky; the glass will answer.

When the sun is set, the west windows go dark and the glass reads by reflection. At night, only the candles speak — strike them and the window glows from within.
