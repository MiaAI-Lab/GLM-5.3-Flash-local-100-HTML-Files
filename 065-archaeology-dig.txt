TITLE: Excavation 42
DESCRIPTION: A stratified dig trench you excavate by hand: a 12-by-10 canvas grid of four soil strata (topsoil, occupation layer, ash lens, sterile clay) that you brush away cell by cell — hold the pointer and dust particles fly while soil thins at stratum-specific hardness — until buried artifacts (amphora, coin, mosaic tessera, bone needle, oil lamp, obsidian point, glass bead, figurine, signet ring) emerge from the pit and then fly up to a linen find tray in the field lab, where each is catalogued with a serif catalogue number and a typewritten label. A depth register tracks your brush against the stratigraphy; a sieve-notes diary narrates the day.
TECHNIQUES:
- Per-cell canvas soil rendering: gradient strata colors, procedural mottle blobs, speckle noise, embedded pebbles, cut-edge highlighting as cells are partially dug
- Artifact emergence scaled by soil removal (reveal value), each artifact drawn as pure canvas geometry (ellipses, beziers, strokes) matching its tray icon
- Dust particle system capped at 130 with gravity and fading life, plus a dashed baulk grid with cm depth ticks
- DOM flight animation: artifact icon clones arc from trench coordinates to the tray rect with a springy cubic-bezier transform
- Typewriter label effect with a blinking caret block; serif catalogue numbers 42-01…42-09
- WebAudio: looping bandpass-filtered noise for brush swish (gated while digging) and a two-note bell chime on filing
INTERACTION:
- Hold the pointer (or spacebar) on a cell to brush; drag to keep digging; harder strata dig slower
- Hover reads out the cell designation, stratum name and depth; the depth meter needle follows your brush
- Fully cleared artifact cells lift the find into the tray automatically; nine finds complete the catalogue
- Field audio toggle enables brush noise and filing chimes; "Re-bury trench" reseeds everything for the next crew
