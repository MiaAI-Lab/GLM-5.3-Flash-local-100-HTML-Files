TITLE: BREATHE
DESCRIPTION: A serene full-screen guided-breathing ritual built on the classic 4-7-8 pattern. A soft peach-to-lavender gradient orb sits at the center of a dawn-colored room, swelling on a four-second inhale, resting through a seven-second hold, and easing down over an eight-second exhale. Concentric lavender ripple rings mark every phase change, an italic serif phase word ("Inhale", "Hold", "Exhale") whispers beneath the orb, and a slim segmented rhythm bar maps 4-7-8 as it fills. Warm-gray micro-typography in wide-tracked light system-ui frames the piece; a dimmer slider lowers the pastel room into candlelight. The signature gesture: press and hold the orb and the breath pauses at the top while a white shimmer ring brightens around it and a soft synthesized shimmer rises.
TECHNIQUES:
- Single CSS radial-gradient orb with layered inset/outset shadows, scaled each frame from a requestAnimationFrame phase machine (easeInOut on 4s/7s/8s timing)
- Concentric ripple rings spawned as ephemeral DOM elements with staggered CSS keyframe animations on each phase boundary
- WebAudio synthesized bell (sine fundamental plus a 2.756x inharmonic partial through a lowpass with exponential decay) marking each phase, plus a four-voice detuned ambient pad through an LFO-swept lowpass filter, started only by explicit user toggle
- Pointer-hold freeze: hold duration is subtracted from phase elapsed time, while a looped bandpass white-noise "shimmer" voice and a glowing ring scale with hold time
- CSS custom-property dimming veil, drifting dust motes, tabular-numeral counters, custom range slider, and a prefers-reduced-motion fallback that swaps scaling for a calm opacity breath and disables ripples
INTERACTION:
- "Begin" (or spacebar) starts and pauses the ritual; the button morphs Begin/Pause/Resume
- Press-and-hold the orb (mouse, touch via pointer capture, or spacebar while focused) to rest at the top of a breath; a shimmer ring and rising airy noise follow the hold; release resumes the count
- The Sound toggle (user-initiated) fades in the ambient pad and enables phase bells; toggling off fades out cleanly
- The Dim slider lowers the pastel room toward candlelight; cycle counter and four dots track completed breaths; the page auto-pauses when the tab is hidden
