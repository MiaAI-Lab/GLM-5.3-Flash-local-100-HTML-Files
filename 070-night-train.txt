TITLE: The Night Train
DESCRIPTION: A journey watched from a sleeper-car window: a wood-framed viewport onto a layered night landscape — twinkling stars, a cratered moon with halo, far and near hill ridges, pine silhouettes in two parallax depths, telegraph poles with sagging wires flicking past, and a moon-path shimmering across a still lake. The warm cabin wraps the glass with curtains, a reading lamp and a ticket-stub card; a throttle lever sweeps parallax speed from stillness to night express, the lamp toggle swaps window clarity for cozy interior glow, the ride toggle synthesizes rail clatter, and tunnels occasionally black out the world. A mono HUD logs distance and the next station.
TECHNIQUES:
- Multi-layer canvas parallax driven by a single speed value: star field with twinkle phases, two sine-generated ridge layers, two pine layers, and pole-and-wire rows each scrolling at distinct wrap rates
- Moon path drawn as horizontal shimmer bars in "lighter" mode with per-bar sine width pulsing over a gradient lake band
- Speed-to-pixel conversion (world units per second) so throttle changes rescale every layer's motion coherently
- Curtain panels and reading lamp as CSS geometry; lamp state re-lights the window with an inset amber wash and lamp halo opacity
- Tunnel system: scheduled and manual tunnel events easing a darkness scalar that dims canvas layers and cross-fades a DOM blackout, with HUD status flip
- WebAudio ride bed: looped lowpassed noise plus double triangle-wave rail clacks, strictly user-initiated
INTERACTION:
- Drag the throttle (0–160 km/h) to slow to a drift or race the night express; every parallax layer and the HUD respond
- Toggle the lamp: interior glow blooms while window contrast deepens
- Open or shut the curtains to frame the view; curtains part with eased rotation
- Press "Enter tunnel" or wait for scheduled tunnels — blackout, muffled HUD, then open line
- Ride sound toggle starts the self-synthesized rail rhythm; distance and next-stop readouts tick as you travel
