TITLE: ASHES & EMBER
DESCRIPTION: A phoenix reborn in particles against a warm black void (#0a0503). Up to ~2,400 embers orbit flickeringly along a procedurally sampled bird silhouette — teardrop body, beak arc, two serrated swept wings, a long forked tail, and trailing flame curls — glowing through an ember palette (#ff6b35/#ffd166/#e63946) with additive halos on the hottest particles. Clicking scatters the whole bird outward with a spark spray and smoke wisps; the embers fall, float, and after a breathless pause begin pulling home — the rebirth — announced by a full-screen italic line, a chime, a rotating haiku, and a counter that remembers every cycle. Holding feeds the fire: heat climbs toward 100%, spawning new embers, enlarging flickers, a charge aura, and a louder crackle bed. Telemetry shows rebirths, ember count, heat %, and the current phase (ROOSTING / ASHFALL / REBIRTH).
TECHNIQUES:
- Phoenix silhouette sampling: ~610 target points from parametric body/half-sine profile, polar head arc, per-side wing arcs with sine serration and f² droop, forked tail taper, and sine flame curls; embers home to targets via (ti·7 + tj) index mapping with jitter
- Three-phase particle life: ROOSTING (flicker orbit + weak home spring + recycle drifters), ASHFALL (gravity + float + slow homing pull), REBIRTH (quadratic pull ramp over 2.6s), with heat coupling rise rates and spawn counts
- Additive rendering: 'lighter' compositing, three-tier heat colors, per-ember radial halos for heatIdx > 0.86, charge aura gradient while holding, radial floor pool glow scaled by heat
- Spark (500 cap, gravity + decay) and smoke (90 cap, expanding radial gradients) systems for scatter events
- WebAudio: looping crackle noise through a lowpass whose gain/frequency track heat; sine whoosh on scatter; four-note rising chime at rebirth
- Mono telemetry (rebirths big numeral, embers, heat %, phase), rotating haiku captions, italic rebirth flash line, tiny caps title with 0.6em tracking
INTERACTION:
- Click the void to scatter the phoenix — embers blast outward with sparks and smoke; watch ASHFALL as they drift and fall
- After ~4 seconds the embers remember: the REBIRTH pulls them home over 2.6 seconds with a chime and a full-screen line; the counter increments and the haiku rotates
- Hold anywhere (or drag while held) to feed the fire: heat rises to 100%, new embers spawn, glow intensifies, the crackle grows; release and it settles
- Space toggles scatter / immediate rebirth; reduced motion slows flicker but keeps phases functional
