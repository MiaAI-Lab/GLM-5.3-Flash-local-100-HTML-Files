TITLE: MIXTAPE '87
DESCRIPTION: A lovingly rendered stereo cassette deck from 1987, presented on a dark desk at life scale: a brushed gunmetal faceplate ("Stera Sound MX-87" in thin wide caps, SOUND·FIELD ×87 brandplate), a recessed cassette bay behind glass with a whole tape inside — dark shell with screws, a cream J-card ruled for handwriting ("for the drive home, vol. 3" plus four track lines, both fully editable in a cursive stack), five-spoke reels, and a brown tape pack that visibly transfers from left to right. Twin cream VU meters carry black needles that dance on the music with proper ballistics, red past-0 arcs, and amber backlight. Six mechanical keys (Rew, Play, Stop, F.Fwd, Eject, Rec) physically depress with 4px travel, hard shadows and a clunk; Play engages amber with a green LED, Rec arms red. The deck plays a self-composed lo-fi loop (Am-F-C-G at 84 bpm, triangle waves with wow-and-flutter detune, dusty noise hats, tape hiss bed), a three-digit amber mechanical counter ticks, and a tape-position scale tracks the side. Eject opens the lid with a servo whirr and the cassette leans out; end of side auto-stops like the real thing.

- Pure-CSS hardware: the deck shell, cassette shell, keys with 3D press travel (translateY + collapsing box-shadow stack), recessed bay with inset glass reflections, brushed-metal gradient flecks, screws with slot highlights
- Cassette internals as DOM: J-card window with repeating-gradient ruled lines and contenteditable cursive title/tracklist, two 5-spoke reels (CSS gradient hub + rotated spoke bars) spun per-frame with counter-rotating speed ratio, and a tape pack band whose reel angles update with tape position
- SVG VU meters: arc scales with tick marks, red overload arc, needles rotated per frame (rotate(-48+value*96) around the pivot) with critically-damped ballistics toward beat-synced targets derived from AudioContext time; amber backlit on cream faces
- WebAudio lo-fi engine: lookahead bar scheduler (120ms interval, 350ms horizon) at 84 bpm — triangle bass/chords through a downward-sweeping lowpass with a sine wow-and-flutter detune LFO per note, noise hats via highpassed buffer, bandpass snare-ish ticks, a looping tape-hiss bed, compressor-bussed; mechanical key clicks and a two-oscillator eject clunk with servo whirr, all user-initiated

INTERACTION:
- Press PLAY: the key physically depresses and latches amber, reels spin, needles dance, hiss rises, and a four-bar lo-fi melody begins; STOP releases everything with a click
- EJECT opens the glass lid with a satisfying clunk and servo whirr; the cassette leans forward; press again to re-seat it (the deck refuses to play while open)
- F.FWD and REW whirl the tape at speed with idle-needle flutter; REW at start or end-of-side auto-stops; the mechanical counter and amber tape scale track every second
- REC arms the red key (and starts play if idle) for louder needles; write your own title and tracklist directly on the J-card (contenteditable, cursive font, red focus ring); spacebar toggles play/stop
