TITLE: Clockwork Orrery
DESCRIPTION: A steampunk brass orrery mounted on aged parchment, drawn as a working instrument. Six lacquered planets ride dashed elliptical orbits around an engraved brass sun with slowly turning rays, seen from 26 degrees above the ecliptic, with a dotted belt of 184 minor planets between Mars and Jupiter. Flanking panels hold a live monospace ephemeris table (period, heliocentric longitude, zodiac house) and a control column with a large draggable brass time dial. Counter-rotating SVG gears bolted to the stage turn at linked speeds as time moves. The signature moment: dragging the dial's bezel scrubs the heavens from 1750 to 2150 — planets whirl through four centuries, the pointer sweeps a ring of decade ticks, the gears counter-rotate, and a soft brass tick sounds at every decade crossed.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas orrery with elliptical Kepler-approximation orbits: mean anomaly plus one-term equation of center, perihelion rotation, focus-offset dashed ellipses, and y-compression for a tilted viewing plane
- Depth-sorted planet rendering with pseudo-3D: lacquered radial-gradient spheres, specular dots, plane shadows, a two-pass Saturn ring (behind and front arcs), and manually letter-spaced engraved labels
- Aged parchment built from stain radials, hairline fiber repeating-gradients and an edge-burn vignette; engraved panel borders with double inset brass shadow
- Draggable circular dial: repeating-conic-gradient tick rings, decade ticks masked in oxblood, a rotating pointer, and a domed brass hub showing year and era
- Linked SVG gear trains whose rotation angles are functions of the year, counter-rotating pairwise
- WebAudio escapement: a 55ms square-wave tick synthesized on decade crossings, created only after the user grabs the dial
INTERACTION:
- Dragging anywhere on the dial's bezel scrubs the years continuously (one full turn spans 1750–2150); the decade ticks click audibly as the pointer passes them
- Arrow keys nudge the dial (±1 year, Shift for ±10), Home and End jump to the range limits; four brass buttons nudge −1/−10/+10/+1 years
- The Engage lever animates time forward at a rate set by the slider (0.2–14.2 years per second), halting automatically at 2150; Return to 1824 resets the mechanism
- The ephemeris table updates live five times a second, planet rows highlight on hover, and everything renders statically but remains fully scrubbable under prefers-reduced-motion

Layout: an engraved header band (over-line "The Gallery of 100 · Room 06 · Instrument in working order", giant small-caps title with an italic brass ampersand, italic subtitle about turning the dial), then a three-panel bench: left ephemeris panel, center orrery stage, right controls; corner flourishes at the page corners.

Center stage: a large canvas (max 760px, aspect 1/0.92) rendering the orrery seen from 26 degrees above the ecliptic (y compressed to 44%). An outer engraved scale ring with 12 zodiac-house labels (ARI, TAU, GEM...) and fine ticks surrounds a soft felt mat. Six planets — Mercurius, Venus, Tellus, Mars, Iuppiter, Saturnus — ride dashed brass ellipses with focus offsets and perihelion rotations; orbit radii use a sqrt-compressed scale so Saturn fits. Between Mars and Jupiter scatter a dotted belt of 184 minor planets. The sun is an engraved brass sphere with a warm glow and 16 slowly rotating rays. Planets are radial-gradient lacquered spheres with specular highlights and plane shadows, drawn in y-sorted pseudo-depth, Saturn carrying a ring drawn in two arcs (behind then front), each with a letter-spaced uppercase engraved label. Bolt two counter-rotating SVG gear clusters to the stage's bottom corners; their angles are linear functions of the year (gear pairs at ±3.2/5.4 and 2.6/7.1 degrees per year), so the whole mechanism visibly turns as time flows.

Left panel — EPHEMERIS: a monospace table with columns Wanderer / Period / Longitude / House; each row has a small gradient swatch sphere, the planet's small-caps name, its period in years, a live heliocentric longitude (e.g. "214.7°") and its zodiac house name in oxblood, updating 5 times per second; a dashed-rule table with hover highlight and an italic apology note about heliocentric longitudes.

Right panel — THE DIAL (signature moment): a large circular brass dial (170–220px) built from a repeating-conic-gradient fine tick ring, an inset ring of oxblood decade ticks, a domed central hub showing the current year and era name (Georgian, Regency, Victorian, Belle Epoque, Machine Age, Atomic, Silicon Dawn, Near Future, Far Solarium), and an oxblood pointer with a brass knob that rotates around the bezel. Dragging the bezel scrubs the year linearly from 1750 to 2150; every decade crossing plays a tiny synthesized brass tick (WebAudio square wave, 55ms, created only on first grab). Below: four engraved brass nudge buttons (−1 yr, −10 yr, +10 yr, +1 yr with 3D press-down active states), a rate slider (0.2–14.2 yr/s with live readout), an Engage/Halt lever that animates the planets and stops at 2150, and a "Return to 1824" button. The dial is a proper role=slider with aria-valuenow/valuetext, arrow-key and Home/End support.

Footer: a brass-ruled foot plate with three tracked uppercase mono lines. Custom brass scrollbar, oxblood selection color and focus rings. Responsive: three panels stack under 820px (ephemeris reflows full-width first), the dial grows to 56vw on phones, flourishes fade on small screens. prefers-reduced-motion freezes the engine but the dial still scrubs with instant redraws.
