The Conductor's Timer
Brass, enamel & walnut · Vienna, c. 1926

Drag the knurled bezel — one notch per minute, a ratchet click each. Then wind it and let it run.

Every second it counts is a second you promised to something. Conductors know: the silence before the downbeat is part of the music.
