TITLE: Neon Botany
DESCRIPTION: A cyberpunk greenhouse deck where luminous cultivars are grown from seed in real time. Planting a Lumen fern, Violet orchid, Signal cactus, or Pulse vine runs a seeded recursive skeleton that visibly unfurls — stems drawn segment by segment, LED nodes pulsing at the joints, blossoms opening in petals of pink and violet with breathing halos. The glasshouse floor is a perspective wireframe grid with reflections, mist blobs drift in screen blend, holographic flickering scanlines sweep the room, and LED ribbons run a slow night-cycle wave along both walls (rate user-controlled). A frosted console offers the seed vault, a nutrient dial that sets growth speed, and holo tags that materialize for each specimen with live growth bars. Sockets on the floor are clickable to plant; fully grown plants can be harvested to free the socket again.
TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural plants: per-seed mulberry32 PRNG grows a recursive skeleton (segments with birth-order normalization, LED nodes, tip blossoms) so every cultivar is unique; growth reveals segments and blossoms progressively by eased birth fraction
- Night-cycle LED waves: ribbons of sine-lit RGB dots along the walls plus a screen-blended luminous band sweeping the floor, both rate-controlled
- Atmosphere: CSS scanlines in multiply, an animated holo sweep bar, canvas mist radials in screen blend, and a cheap vertical-smear reflection of stems on the wet floor
- Holographic UI: flickering translucent tags with mono specimen codes (e.g. LMN-0FA3), segment/bloom counts and animated growth bars; glassmorphism panes with backdrop blur
- Interaction model: click canvas sockets to plant (nearest-socket hit test), click grown plants to harvest; keyboard 1-4 plants cultivars; rAF loop paused on tab-hide; reduced-motion static frame with re-render on interaction
INTERACTION:
- Press a seed button (or keys 1-4) to plant that cultivar in the first free socket and watch it grow before you; the deck notes how many sockets remain
- Click an empty socket on the floor to plant the currently selected cultivar there
- Click a fully grown plant to harvest it — its holo tag is removed and the socket reopens
- Drag the nutrient dial to speed or slow all growth (5-100%); drag the wave dial to set the night-cycle LED tempo from glacial to strobe
- Holo tags flicker in holographic cyan and track each plant's live growth percentage; reduced motion renders a calm still greenhouse with full functionality on interaction
