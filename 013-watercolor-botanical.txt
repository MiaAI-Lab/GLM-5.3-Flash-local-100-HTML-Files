TITLE: HERBARIUM
DESCRIPTION: A watercolor botanical study laid out like a museum plate: a large peony specimen built from layered SVG petals with feGaussianBlur washes and irregular organic edges, two fern fronds, paint bleeds, faint pencil construction lines, and graphite italic annotations with dashed leader lines. The paper (#f8f4ea) carries a feTurbulence grain overlay and soft stains; a rotated specimen label is held by two strips of #e6dcc3 tape. Greens #7d9b76/#4a6741, blossom pinks #d98c9f/#b05f78, graphite #5b5b5b, and a whisper of stamen gold. Typography is ui-serif italic for annotations and letterpress caps for the Latin name. Signature moments: hovering the blossom makes each petal spring open with overshoot; a watering-can toggle re-wets the paint so saturation and bleeds deepen like wet media; clicking anywhere on the paper sows a new small blossom that blooms with a springy entrance.
TECHNIQUES:
- Layered inline SVG watercolor: five-plus-six-plus-three nested petal rings from two hand-drawn petal paths via &lt;use&gt;, filled with linear/radial wash gradients and softened by feGaussianBlur filters at three strengths
- feTurbulence paper grain overlaid full-page with multiply blending; translucent bleed ellipses and paths that deepen when wet
- CSS transform-box/transform-origin petal anatomy: attribute rotations on wrapper groups so CSS hover transforms (springy cubic-bezier overshoot) compose instead of override
- Wet-paint state via a body class driving SVG filter (saturate/contrast), bleed-layer opacity, and a blurred damp halo
- Click-to-sow: SVGPoint transformed by getScreenCTM().inverse() to place cloned #seedling blossoms with a scale-from-zero spring entrance
- Botanical-plate editorial grid: vertical spine title (writing-mode), running head, taped label card with dashed tape edges, letterpress text-shadow, graphite leader lines with arrowheads
INTERACTION:
- Hover the peony to make all petals lift, rotate a few degrees, and scale with a springy overshoot curve; move away and they settle
- Click "Water the paint" (or its state caption changes) to wet the pigment: colors saturate and deepen, bleeds strengthen, a damp halo appears; click again to let it dry
- Click anywhere on the paper to sow a new small blossom at that exact point — up to 24 — each with a random rotation and scale, blooming with a springy entrance
- The tally of adventitious blooms fills six pink dots and an italic note ("a stray bloom, sown by hand"); the plate hint fades after the first sowing
- Reduced motion disables petal springs and sowing animation (blooms appear placed); focus-visible dotted outlines on controls
