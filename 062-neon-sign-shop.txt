TITLE: Neon &amp; Sons
DESCRIPTION: A bespoke neon workshop at night: a CSS brick wall lit by two warm wall sconces, a hanging wooden shop board, and a working glass-tube neon sign that the visitor designs live — typing in the job ticket re-bends the tube lettering instantly as layered SVG strokes (blurred halo, gas-glow mid line, bright glass core). The sign can be switched between flowing script and heavy caps, refilled with five gas colors, given a flicker personality, dragged anywhere on the brick, and powered with a transformer toggle that starts a self-synthesized mains hum; one letter is always a little faulty and stutters dark with a buzz. A taped handwritten note from the master bender and a price tag finish the set dressing.
TECHNIQUES:
- Pure-CSS brick wall built from three conic-gradient layers forming a running-bond pattern with mortar, plus vignette and horizontal brick shading overlays
- Neon tube rendering as three stacked SVG text layers with different stroke widths and feGaussianBlur filters, core stroke mixed toward white for the glass filament
- Per-character tspan splitting so one pseudo-randomly chosen faulty letter can flicker independently of the whole sign
- Flicker state machine with four patterns (steady sine, slow breathe, nervous random dropouts, faulty brown-outs) easing master opacity across all layers and the wall glow
- WebAudio transformer hum (detuned 60/180 Hz sawtooths through a lowpass with LFO wobble) and bandpass sawtooth buzz bursts timed to the faulty letter's dark frames
- Pointer-capture drag with velocity-based sway rotation and keyboard arrow nudging; sconce pools, dust, and sawdust bench as CSS gradients
INTERACTION:
- Type in the tube-text field and watch the glass tube re-bend letter by letter in script or caps
- Pick a gas fill swatch; the tube color, filament core, and the glow wash on the brick all retint
- Choose a flicker pattern; the faulty letter stutters on its own schedule with audible buzz when powered
- Drag the sign (or use arrow keys on it) to hang it anywhere on the wall; it sways with your hand
- Toggle the transformer to start the hum and hear the flicker; everything else works unpowered but silent
