TITLE: Flora Incognita
DESCRIPTION: A botanist's field notebook laid on the desk: three pressed-specimen sheets — Silena vesperis (meadow catchfly), Aquilegia incognita (unknown columbine), Papaver errans (wanderer's poppy) — each with Latin binomial headings, herbarium stamps, taped corners, and a live canvas figure that grows through its whole lifecycle. Hovering a sheet plays its season from seed to sprout, leaf, bud, bloom and fruit; the ruled timeline beneath each sheet can be scrubbed by hand, paused, and read backwards. Dated italic observations sit on ruled lines, a watercolor swatch panel shows the meadow's pigments as paint daubs with codes, a brass-ringed loupe is dragged over the sheets to magnify the living plant in real time, and a wooden ruler crowns the desk. All procedural botany, no images.
TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural lifecycle botany on Canvas 2D: a single lifecycle parameter t drives seed, radicle, growing curved stem (with stem hairs under the loupe), unfurling leaves along the stem, bowing bud with sepals, opening bloom (three petal architectures — notched catchfly, spurred columbine with spur strokes, crinkled poppy cup), and fruit (ridged capsule or splayed follicles with scattered seed)
- Deterministic easing per stage with overlap windows so growth flows continuously; sway only while the season plays
- Scrubable timeline per sheet: range input styled as a botanist's scale with stage markers, synchronized both ways with the animation and the stage/day readout
- Draggable loupe: brass-ringed CSS lens with a canvas eye that re-renders the plant beneath at 2.1x zoom (higher detail — stamens and stem hairs appear only under magnification), with crosshair and live mono readout
- Paper scenography in CSS: taped corners, rotated sheets, rubber-stamp roundel, ruled observation lines via repeating gradients, watercolor daubs as border-radius blobs with inner shading, twine bow in inline SVG
- Sheet-local rAF budgeting: plants animate only while hovered (or under the loupe); the master loop also drives the loupe; tab-hide pauses the loop
INTERACTION:
- Hover a specimen sheet and its whole lifecycle plays — sprout, leaf, bud, bloom, fruit — along the ruled timeline; leave and it pauses where it stands
- Scrub any timeline by hand to move the season forward or backward; typing a stage stops autoplay
- Click a canvas to play/pause; keyboard Enter/Space steps the lifecycle in jumps; every slider and canvas is focusable with visible focus rings
- Drag the brass loupe anywhere on the desk; rest it over a sheet to magnify the living plant (finer detail appears under the glass) and read its name, stage and day in the lens
- Reduced motion: no hover autoplay — scrub, click-step and the loupe still work

Then three specimen sheets in a rotated, taped grid, each containing: Latin name + common name + sheet number between rules; a bordered live canvas stage with dashed soil line and "fig. n — live" label (sheet 1 also carries a round herbarium stamp); a mono stage caption ("SEED · day 0" / "0%"); a scrubable range timeline with markers SEED SPROUT LEAF BUD BLOOM FRUIT; an italic observation line on ruled background; a mono morphometrics row. Beneath, a two-panel band: OBSERVATIONS with a twine-bow SVG and two dated italic paragraphs on ruled lines; and PIGMENTS OF THE MEADOW — eight watercolor daubs (irregular border-radius blobs with highlight/sheen, hover lift) labeled Leaf Green FL-01 through Loam FL-08, plus a hand-ground note.

The plants: each sheet's canvas draws its species from a lifecycle parameter t in [0,1]. Seed: a loam-colored ellipse with a sprouting radicle. Stem: quadratic-curved stroke rising by eased t with species height/curve constants; hairs drawn only when magnified. Leaves: 4–5 alternate leaves unfurling upward at increasing thresholds, midrib vein. Bud: sepals and a bowing ellipse that straightens as it swells. Bloom: radial petals — catchfly five notched petals with gradient deep-to-light, columbine five petals each with a drawn nectar spur, poppy four broad crinkled cup petals with a dark center — opening over t=0.60–0.84 and wilting after 0.88. Fruit: poppy ridged capsule or columbine/catchfly splayed follicles with scattered seed dots.

Signature moment: hovering a sheet plays the plant's whole lifecycle — seed, sprout, leaf, bud, bloom, fruit — along the timeline; scrubbing works in both directions; and the draggable brass loupe magnifies whatever sheet it rests on at 2.1x with extra detail (stamens, stem hairs) visible only under the glass, reading out "S. vesperis · BLOOM d58" in the lens.

Engineering: one style block, one script block, no external resources, system fonts, custom selection/scrollbar colors, devicePixelRatio-capped canvases, pointer events with capture on the loupe, master rAF loop paused on tab-hide, responsive 3-2-1 column collapse, and a reduced-motion mode with no hover autoplay while scrubbing, stepping, and the loupe remain fully functional.
