TITLE: Danseurs
DESCRIPTION: A mechanical music box resting in a dark walnut case on a dim stage. A brass cylinder pinned with silver studs turns past a steel comb, plucking a lilting lullaby note by note — each pin flashing as it lifts a tooth, ivory note glyphs drifting up from the strike. A ballerina silhouette in ivory tutu waltzes atop a spinning brass disc in the velvet-lined right wing. Controls are the instrument itself: a brass wind-up key you hold to fill the mainspring (a needle gauge shows tension), a slow-motion toggle that reveals the mechanism, two cylinder tunes to swap, and a sound lamp. The signature moment is the hold-to-wind, release-to-play ritual: tension you store with your own hand becomes the tempo the box spends.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas mechanism: gradient-shaded brass cylinder with scrolling spiral hatch, ten labeled comb teeth (D4–F5) with silver tips, and silver pin studs positioned per-tune that ride toward the comb
- Pluck feedback: tooth flash (white bloom with canvas shadowBlur), drifting quarter-note glyphs with sine wobble, and a three-partial synthesized pluck (sine fundamental + 2.01x + 3.02x partials with fast attack, 1.5s decay) for a music-box timbre
- Hold-to-wind interaction via pointer capture plus keyboard hold (Space/Enter), ratchet tick synthesis every 160ms while winding, and tempo tied to stored tension (0.45–1.0 of nominal)
- SVG ballerina in fifth-position arms and passé leg, rotating via transform attribute; brass key icon spins while winding; needle gauge rotates −90°..+90° with tension
- CSS material craft: wood-grain overlay from crossed repeating gradients, velvet interior from 45° nap stripes with brass inset frame and corner fittings, engraved brass plates with screw details
- Reduced-motion fallback: static renders on an interval, no waltz spin, no mote drift; all audio strictly after user gesture
INTERACTION:
- Press and hold the brass key (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) to wind — the ratchet ticks, the needle climbs, the key spins; release below a quarter-turn and the box teases you to try again
- Release the key to play: the cylinder advances through the pinned tune, pins flash and pluck, the ballerina waltzes, and the tune loops until the spring runs down and the box falls silent
- Toggle Slow mechanism to watch pins ride the comb at a third speed with true pitch; swap between Berceuse en sol and Valse tendre cylinders (when not playing); mute the sound lamp
- Live engraved-plate captions narrate each state change; layout collapses to a single velvet column with the ballerina above the movement on phones
