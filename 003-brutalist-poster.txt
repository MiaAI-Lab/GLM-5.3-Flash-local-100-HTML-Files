TITLE: Concrete Poetry
DESCRIPTION: A neo-brutalist interactive poster zine on raw warning-yellow: massive 900-weight black caps with a hollow-stroke display line, thick 4px black borders and hard 8px offset shadows everywhere, two opposing marquee strips of slogans, a house-rules ledger, a stat panel of oversized numerals, and a field of draggable sticker chips. The signature moments: five stickers that can be dragged anywhere with velocity-based rotation physics and a springy settle, and a red Slogan Dispenser block that stamps a fresh random slogan with a heavy thud animation and keeps count.
TECHNIQUES:
- Neo-brutalist system: uniform 4px solid black borders, hard offset box-shadows (8px/4px, never blurred), zero border-radius, paper and black panel inversions
- Display typography at font-weight 900 with negative tracking, a transparent-fill line drawn via -webkit-text-stroke hollow outlines, and one red accent word
- Draggable stickers built on pointer capture, converting percent home positions to pixels on first grab, with velocity read from pointer deltas
- Rotation physics: sticker tilt follows drag velocity with damping, clamped to ±28°, then a rAF settle loop with friction and wall bounce before re-anchoring rotation
- Dual marquee strips using duplicated content and translateX(-50%) keyframes, the lower one reversed on a red ground
- Stamp animation: scale-2.1-to-1 overshoot cubic-bezier with blur and rotate keyframes, restarted via class remove/void-offsetWidth/class add
INTERACTION:
- Grab any of the five stickers and drag — it tilts in the direction of travel like a card pulled through honey, keeps momentum with friction and wall bounces on release, then settles to rest; arrow keys nudge stickers for keyboard users
- Click, Enter, or Space the red Slogan Dispenser to stamp one of sixteen rotating slogans with a thud overshoot and an incrementing ×N counter
- Hovering house-rule rows slides them right on yellow; marquees scroll continuously in opposite directions
- Visible 4px black focus outlines, selection styled black-on-yellow, reduced-motion disables marquees, stamp animation and settle physics
PROMPT:
Build a single-file HTML neo-brutalist poster zine titled "CONCRETE POETRY". Ground: raw yellow #f6f61a everywhere. Ink: pure black #000 for borders, type and shadows; one clashing red #ff3b30 block; near-black #1d1d1f and paper #fffef5 for panel inversions. Every border in the entire page is exactly 4px solid black; every shadow is a hard offset (8px 8px 0 black, or 4px 4px 0 for small elements), never blurred, never colored. Zero rounded corners anywhere. All type is system-ui at font-weight 900, uppercase for display, with tight negative tracking on big sizes.

Masthead (sticky, yellow, 4px bottom border): a small black bordered chip "Nº 07 / ISSUE" at left, a giant centered wordmark "CONCRETE×POETRY" with the × in red (clamp 2rem–5.4rem), and a rotated paper tag "DRAG THE STICKERS · CLICK THE RED" at right. Below, a black marquee strip with yellow 900-weight letter-spaced slogans (FORM FOLLOWS FURY ★ LESS IS A BORE ★ CONCRETE REMEMBERS) scrolling left; later, a second red-ground marquee with black text scrolling right.

Main field: two-column grid. Left column: a display headline "LOUD IS A LOAD-BEARING WALL" at clamp(3rem–9.5rem), line-height 0.86, where "LOAD-" is hollow (transparent fill, 3px black text stroke) and "WALL" is red; a bold dek paragraph with a black-highlighted phrase; and a paper stat panel with 4px border and hard shadow split into three cells — "4px / BORDER WEIGHT", "900 / ONLY WEIGHT" (inverted black cell), "0 / ROUNDED CORNERS" (red cell) — numerals at clamp(1.9rem–3.4rem).

Right column stack: the red Slogan Dispenser — 4px bordered red block with hard shadow, an ×N counter chip in the corner, heading "SLOGAN DISPENSER", a stamp line area at least 3.2em tall, and a paper chip "HIT ME AGAIN". Signature moment #2: clicking (or Enter/Space on it, since it is a focusable role=button) stamps a random slogan from sixteen authored lines like "SILENCE IS A LAYOUT BUG." and "TASTE IS FEAR WEARING A SCARF." — the text slams in from scale 2.1 with blur, overshoots to 0.94, settles with a small counter-rotation, while the whole block presses down into its shadow (transform translate(6px,6px), shadow shrinks) for a physical thud. Below it, a paper "HOUSE RULES" ledger with a black header bar and four numbered rules ("YELLOW IS A VOLUME, NOT A COLOR." — "RED APPEARS ONCE PER PAGE. USE IT LIKE A FIRE ALARM."), rows sliding right 6px on hover with a yellow flash.

Signature moment #1: five absolutely-positioned sticker chips scattered over the field (black/red/paper variants, each with a small tracked sub-label like "I DARE", "STILL WET", "NO RADIUS", "ON PURPOSE", "ZINE NO. 07"): DRAG ME, FRESHLY STAMPED, 4PX FOREVER, ANTI-GRACEFUL, NOISE IS SIGNAL. Each is grabbable via pointer capture, keeps its percent home converted to pixels, tilts up to ±28° following drag velocity with damping, and on release glides with momentum (0.86 friction), bounces off field edges at half restitution, then eases its rotation back into a settled state. Arrow keys nudge any focused sticker.

Footer: black bar with yellow text, a hazard-stripe divider built from repeating-linear-gradient, and mono-spaced-feeling tracked uppercase colophon lines. Custom scrollbar: yellow track with 4px black left border and solid black square thumb. Selection is black-on-yellow. Focus is a 4px black outline. Responsive: field collapses to one column under 900px, stat cells stack under 560px, masthead tag hides on phones. prefers-reduced-motion stops both marquees, the stamp animation and the settle physics. No images, no fonts, no scripts beyond one inline block — everything is borders, shadows and weight.
