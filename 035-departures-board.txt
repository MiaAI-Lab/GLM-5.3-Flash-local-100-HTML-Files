TITLE: GATE 12
DESCRIPTION: A split-flap departures hall rendered as a wall of amber tiles on near-black lacquer inside a dark terminal foyer. Eight flight rows carry fourteen destination flap cells, three huge airport-code tiles, five time tiles and one wide status tile; every character change is a true falling-flap animation with mechanical stagger and a synthesized clack hail. A soft ceiling-light gradient falls over the frame, DELAYED and FINAL CALL blink, and a console beneath holds a filter field, auto-shuffle and sound controls. Typing blanks every non-matching row with a rolling flip; clearing the field flips the whole schedule back.
TECHNIQUES:
- Per-character split-flap engine: cyclic character-set sequences capped at eight steps, rAF-driven rotateX keyframes with a midpoint letter swap, per-column and per-row delays, blank-tile styling for spaces
- Fully synthesized WebAudio: rate-limited square-wave clack per flap step and a triangle thud per row event, all behind a user-initiated audio toggle
- Grid-based board chrome: tile split-line pseudo-element, amber text-shadow glow, ceiling radial falloff, inset-lacquer frame, legend chips with blink animation for DELAYED
- Search-as-theater filtering: non-matching rows blank themselves cell by cell while matches stay lit; clearing restores via a full staggered board reshuffle; live next-departure countdown and stats
INTERACTION:
- Type in the filter field (or press /) to blank out non-matching rows and highlight matches by city, code or flight number; clear it to flip everything back
- Click any row to send it to a fresh world destination; Auto mode (A) reshuffles one or two rows every few seconds; Reshuffle (R) re-deals the entire board; Sound (M) toggles the clatter
- The hall clock ticks each second, DELAYED rows blink, and the footer tracks flights shown, next departure countdown, flap count and auto state
