TITLE: FIREFLY FIELD
DESCRIPTION: A summer-night forest clearing rendered on canvas: a green-black gradient sky, a cratered waxing-gibbous moon with a soft halo, swaying grass silhouettes, drifting mist, tree shadows, and dozens of fireflies blinking in organic, non-repeating rhythms. A glass jar waits at the edge of the grass. Drag it near a firefly and it spooks — darting away with a burst of speed — so catching one means approaching slowly and letting the light drift close. Caught fireflies dim and mill inside the glass; a Release all button (or a click on the jar) swirls them back into the night. An optional bandpassed cricket ambience chirps irregularly; field notes and a live free/jarred counter frame the scene.
TECHNIQUES:
- Organic blink synthesis: each firefly's brightness combines a slow multi-frequency sine "breath" with a sharp sinusoid stutter (pow 6) so flashes never repeat on a grid, dimmed by a fear factor
- Fear/curiosity steering: proximity to the jar or pointer builds a spook scalar that drives escape acceleration and speed cap (240 px/s vs 90 px/s calm), decaying exponentially; a held jar instead attracts glowing flies within 190 px
- Additive radial-gradient glow sprites (hot #fef08a core above 0.72 brightness, cool #d9f99d below) with a small solid core dot; jarred flies render as a trembling grid of faint lights inside the glass outline
- Environment layers: precomputed tree silhouettes, two-frequency swaying grass blades sorted by depth, ellipse-stretched radial mist blobs, procedural starfield, and radial-gradient moon with maria dots
INTERACTION:
- Drag the jar anywhere (pointer events with capture); release the button on a touch, click the jar to grab it; fireflies flee as it nears and enter the glass when held still near them
- Click the jar (or press Release all) to swirl every caught firefly free with upward escape velocities
- The Crickets toggle starts a self-synthesized ambience (WebAudio square-wave chirp trains through a bandpass filter, randomized timing, user-initiated only)
