The glacier kept the valley for four hundred years, and the valley kept its records for one: a line of instruments, a line of photographs, a line of painted rocks marking where the tongue reached each summer. Scrubs of the century show the rocks losing their post one by one.

Nothing here is dramatic if you watch it daily. It is dramatic the way interest is dramatic — small percentages, patient arithmetic, a lake where your grandmother's bridge stood. Drag the slider slowly. The ice answers in decades, not afternoons.
