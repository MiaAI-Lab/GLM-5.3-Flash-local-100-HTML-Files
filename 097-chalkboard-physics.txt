TITLE: CHALK & CHALKDUST
DESCRIPTION: A professor's chalkboard that teaches projectile motion by rewriting itself. The board (deep green #2e4a3f in a wooden #8a6540 frame with a chalk tray holding two sticks and an eraser) is a canvas of hand-wobbly chalk strokes: a cannon sketched at the launch angle with a yellow θ arc, a dotted white trajectory with jittered dots, dashed apex drop-line with h annotation, a bracketed range R, and a yellow velocity vector — plus corner titles ("PROJECTILE MOTION — R = v²·sin(2θ)/g", current v/g, and an italic "fig. the perfect forty-five"). Below, paper cards hold three chalk-styled sliders (angle θ, muzzle speed v, gravity g with Earth/Moon/Mars presets), dashed readout boxes (Range/Apex/Flight), and the derivation revealed line-by-line (i–v) with a handwritten professor's note in a marker font. Every slider change rewrites the math in chalk and lobs a fresh yellow projectile along the new arc with a chalk-dust puff on landing; Erase board sweeps ghostly smudges and floating dust; dust motes drift over the whole hall via feTurbulence.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas chalk rendering: multi-segment lines with per-vertex random jitter (chalkLine), double-pass text for chalk texture (chalkText), dashed construction lines, angle arc, range brackets, and ground hatching
- Projectile physics: closed-form T = 2v·sinθ/g, R = v²·sin(2θ)/g, h = (v·sinθ)²/2g; live integration of a lobbed projectile with adjustable playback rate scaled by flight time, ground shadow ellipse, and impact dust puff
- Derivation choreography: reveal counter advances over time after each change (steps i–v fade in as annotations appear: apex line, R bracket, T label), results re-filled in green chalk accents
- Eraser behavior: random ghost-smudge radial gradients + dust bursts; preset chips (Earth 9.81 / Moon 1.62 / Mars 3.71) rewrite gravity and the arc
- Paper UI: sliders with dotted chalk tracks, dashed readout boxes, serif italic labels, handwritten (Segoe Print stack) professor notes, wood-grain frame with inset highlights, feTurbulence dust overlay at 5% alpha
INTERACTION:
- Drag θ (10–80°), v (8–55 m/s), or g (1.6–24.8 m/s²): the derivation re-inks, the dotted trajectory redraws, and a new projectile lobs along the arc — foot message narrates each rewrite
- Earth/Moon/Mars chips flip gravity and puff chalk dust; Erase board (or E) sweeps smudges; Solve live toggles hold/auto mode; ↑/↓ nudge the angle
- Watch the apex annotation, range bracket, and flight-time label chalk themselves in as the reveal counter advances
- The readouts and derivation results (green chalk accents) update to the exact closed-form numbers; the professor's note rotates through four aphorisms
