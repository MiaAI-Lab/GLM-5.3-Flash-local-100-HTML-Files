TITLE: Field Sketch
DESCRIPTION: A traveler's sketchbook lying open on a dark desk: two hand-drawn spreads (Lisbon, then Kyoto) rendered as wobbly SVG pencil drawings that bloom into muted watercolor when hovered, washi-taped photo placeholders built entirely from CSS gradients, italic margin annotations, a stamped date, a coffee-ring stain, and a page corner that curls on hover and turns the spread with a full 3D flip. Torn-paper page templates clone into a static left page, a static right page, and a rotating leaf whose back face carries the next spread's first page.
TECHNIQUES:
- SVG hand-drawn aesthetic via feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap wobble filters applied to layered stroke groups
- Pencil-to-watercolor reveal: grayscale(1) filter on the sketch group transitioning to full color while hidden wash layers (translucent teal/ochre/vermilion rects and paths) fade in on hover
- 3D page turn with preserve-3d leaf, backface-hidden front/back faces, a glossy sheen sweep, and template cloning to swap spread content at the midpoint
- CSS-art ephemera: running-bond-free paper texture from repeating gradients, washi tape strips, taped photo cards with painted-gradient thumbnails, a blurred double-ring coffee stain
- Rubber-stamp date badge with radial mask erosion; dashed perspective guides in the sketches
- Two named dots in the masthead track the current city spread
INTERACTION:
- Hover or keyboard-focus any sketch to watch the pencil drawing remember its colors
- Click the page corner (or the leaf, or press Enter/Space) to turn the spread with a 3D flip; a second turn brings you back
- On narrow screens the book becomes a single page and the flip still carries you to the next page
- The corner curl lifts toward your pointer before the turn
