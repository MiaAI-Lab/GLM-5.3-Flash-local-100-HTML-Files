TITLE: Coral Garden
DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed living reef aquarium rendered on Canvas 2D: swaying anemones and sea fans built from sine-driven bezier tentacles, three species of boid fish that school, wander and flee the cursor, columns of wobbling bubbles, and animated light caustics dancing across a speckled sand floor. A serif exhibit plate sits top-left like a museum label, a mono species card top-right, and a keeper's bench at the bottom carries the environmental narrative: a "warm the water" toggle slowly bleaches every coral to bone white while the temperature readout climbs, and a "restore the reef" button lets cool currents bring the colour back.
TECHNIQUES:
- Boids flocking simulation (separation, alignment, cohesion) with cursor-avoidance and food-seeking sprint behaviour
- Procedural coral drawing: quadratic-bezier anemone tentacles with per-tendril phase sway, recursive branching coral, arced sea-fan ribs, tube sponges and wavy brain-coral contours
- Animated caustics via layered sine polylines composited in "lighter" mode over a gradient sand dune with procedural speckles
- Colour-lerp bleaching system mixing every coral colour toward bone white, with tentacle droop and reduced sway
- Layered gradients: water column, surface shimmer, cursor dive-light in screen blend, radial vignette
- WebAudio ambience: filtered noise water hiss with randomly scheduled sine plinks, all user-initiated
INTERACTION:
- Click (or press F) to drop food pellets that sink; the whole school sprints to swarm them and nibbles pellets away
- Move the pointer to act as a dive light; fish steer away from it
- Toggle "warm the water" to bleach the reef over several seconds; "Restore the reef" reverses it; B toggles too
- Sound button enables self-synthesized underwater ambience and feeding plinks
