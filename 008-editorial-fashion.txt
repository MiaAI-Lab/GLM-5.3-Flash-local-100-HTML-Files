TITLE: Maison Édition 04
DESCRIPTION: A high-fashion editorial spread on ivory paper. An oversized italic serif masthead opens an asymmetric two-column grid of "duotone photographs" built entirely from CSS gradients with film grain — an oxblood pit gown plate, an ink portrait, a mustard pleat study — each carrying a vertical caption, a folio number, and a hidden pull-quote drawer that slides up like turning a magazine page on hover. A cover story with drop cap, an editor's letter, an "In this édition" index with oxblood numerals, a horizontally scrollable filmstrip of nine looks with snap scrolling, hairline rules and a colophon complete the number.
TECHNIQUES:
- Duotone photography synthesized from layered radial and repeating-linear gradients (oxblood #6e1423, ink #141210, mustard #c99a2e) with an overlay-blended grain speckle
- Page-turn pull-quote: gradient-faded ink drawer translated 101% below each plate, easing up on hover/focus with a long cubic-bezier
- Duotone swap on hover via hue-rotate/saturate/contrast filter plus a slow scale breath on the plate
- Editorial furniture: vertical-rl rotated captions, mono folio numbers, hairline rules, drop cap in oxblood, 2.5px masthead rules
- Filmstrip with scroll-snap, custom thin scrollbar, and wheel-to-horizontal translation with edge release so the page keeps scrolling at the ends
- Fixed paper-grain overlay of sparse radial flecks and hairline repeating gradients across the whole page
INTERACTION:
- Hovering (or keyboard-focusing) any photograph re-grades its duotone toward cooler reds, breathes the image scale 3.5%, and slides the pull-quote drawer up from the bottom like a turning page
- The filmstrip scrolls horizontally via drag, trackpad, snap points, Arrow/Home/End keys when focused, and a vertical mouse wheel that converts to horizontal until the strip ends, then hands scrolling back to the page
- Index rows, bylines and look captions offer quiet hover states; selection is oxblood-on-ivory with an ink scrollbar
- Layout reflows to a single column with the text column first on tablets, and the duo grid stacks on phones
