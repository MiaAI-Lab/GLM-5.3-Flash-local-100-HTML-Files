TITLE: DOME 5
DESCRIPTION: An observatory night-shift instrument. The left viewport is a dome interior rendered on canvas: a dark room with ribbed walls, a floor platform, and a telescope tube aimed by the user; a rounded slit in the dome opens onto a live starfield whose stars and Messier targets shift with azimuth/altitude. Dragging slews the telescope (the dome slit lags behind with mechanical inertia); when the amber reticle closes within 2.4° of one of twelve catalogued targets it locks with pulsing corner brackets, and Enter (or the button) drops the view into a full-screen magnified eyepiece — a breath-held smoothstep zoom from dome room to deep sky, resolving into a procedurally drawn object (Andromeda with dust lane, Orion with Trapezium, Pleiades with diffraction crosses, globular of 300 stars, ring, dumbbell, spiral arms, Sombrero edge-on). Exposure counts up, the observation logs to the session list, and the target strikes green in the Messier queue. Red-light night mode re-inks the whole console in preservation red; an optional servo sound rises with slew speed. Palette: dome #14161d, instrument #1f232b, red mode #7a0e0e-family, eyepiece #050810. Type: mono targeting readouts, serif plaque.
TECHNIQUES:
- Canvas dome scene: radial-gradient walls, clipped rounded-rect slit with lagging rotation (spring-damper on azimuth error), per-star brightness/twinkle mapped from real az/alt coordinates, target glow blobs with live angular-distance labels
- Telescope drawn as a tapered polygon tube with rings, finder, slew LED (red while moving, green parked) and mount ellipse; reticle with pulsing lock brackets
- Eyepiece zoom: smoothstep-driven radial star-streak burst that crossfades into one of eight procedural deep-sky renderers (gradient galaxies with dust lanes, trapezium nebula wisps, 300-point pow(2.4) globular, diffraction-crossed Pleiades, radial ring gradient, dumbbell lobes, two-arm spiral dots, Sombrero ellipse)
- Session state: lock detection by angular distance, exposure timer, log list DOM insertion with slide-in animation, seen-state green strike in the target queue
- Red-light mode via CSS custom-property theme swap (body.redmode) mirrored into canvas colors
- WebAudio: sawtooth servo through lowpass whose gain and pitch track slew rate; sine blips on lock/enter/log; 54 Hz hum in the eyepiece
INTERACTION:
- Drag the dome viewport (or arrow keys; Shift for fine steps) to slew the telescope — readouts show AZ/ALT/RATE live
- Click a Messier row for an automatic slew to that target
- When the reticle locks (within 2.4°), press Enter or "Enter eyepiece" for the zoom; Esc or "Retract" pulls back out
- "Night vision" flips the entire console to red-light mode; "Servo sound" enables mechanical slew audio
- Twelve observations fill the queue (seen targets turn green, "logged"); the session log timestamps every eyepiece entry
