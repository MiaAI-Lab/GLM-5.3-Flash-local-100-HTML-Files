TITLE: DARKROOM
DESCRIPTION: A photographer's darkroom portfolio bathed in red safelight. A serif masthead names the studio; film-strip sprocket borders run down both edges of the screen. The centrepiece is a 3×3 contact sheet of nine silver-gelatine "prints" that hang undeveloped — blurred, sepia, nearly black — until the visitor hovers one and the image resolves before their eyes, grain settling into full-contrast monochrome. Clicking a developed frame pins it to a glowing light table below with a typewritten caption. Beside the sheet, an enlarger bay (built entirely from CSS shapes) projects onto an easel: switch the enlarger on, press Expose, and an eight-second amber timer counts down a projection that fades in on the paper. Typewritten captions, serif name, amber timer glow, and a red vignette you can switch off.
TECHNIQUES:
- Fake photography: nine pure-CSS gradient "negatives" (harbour fog, stairwell, portrait, orchard, dunes, sea stack, window light, cables, mist) with no images anywhere
- Development effect: filter transition from sepia(0.85) blur(7px) brightness(0.42) contrast(0.62) to neutral, layered under an SVG feTurbulence grain overlay whose opacity fades as the print resolves
- CSS-built enlarger: rail, head with vents, lens cone (clip-path), volumetric beam (gradient wedge), baseboard and easel, with a pulsing lamp glow during exposure
- requestAnimationFrame countdown timer with tenth-of-a-second display, film sprockets via repeating radial-gradient backgrounds, and WebAudio noise-buffer shutter clicks plus a two-tone develop chime
INTERACTION:
- Hovering (or keyboard-focusing) any contact-sheet print develops it in real time; clicking pins it to the light table (max six, oldest is bumped) with a typewriter-style caption reveal; clicking a pinned print removes it
- The Safelight toggle kills the red ambience; the Enlarger toggle lights the lamp and beam and arms the Expose button
- Expose runs the 8-second countdown during which the projected scene fades onto the easel paper, increments the exposure counter, and sounds a soft chime
