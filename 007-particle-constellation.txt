TITLE: Starlight Loom
DESCRIPTION: A dark interactive starfield where drifting particles weave luminous proximity threads with one another and with the cursor — the cursor is a glowing needle that gathers the mesh around the hand. Threads grade from sky-blue #7dd3fc to violet #c4b5fd across the weave, drawn additively over faint nebula washes. Clicking pins a four-pointed anchor star that other particles string themselves to; dragging a pin pulls the whole web taut; double-clicking releases a shimmer burst of sparks and a rose ripple. A glass pill console tunes thread tension and particle density, scatters the field, seeds seven pins, and toggles a soft synthesized chime. A thin-tracked uppercase title and serif italic subtitle float in the center of the loom.
TECHNIQUES:
- Additive-blend canvas rendering (globalCompositeOperation lighter) for star points, glow gradients and luminous threads
- Proximity thread mesh with spatial hash grid (120px cells) so particle-to-particle links stay O(n) at up to 190 particles
- Gradient thread coloring by screen position — sky-blue toward the left, violet toward the lower right — with alpha quadratic in proximity
- Cursor needle: radial glow halo, ringed pulse, distance-falloff attraction steering particles within 220px
- Pinned anchor stars with four-point flares, dual-sourced threads to every nearby particle and pin-to-pin wires, and taut-pull physics applied in the simulation step
- Spark bursts with drag, gravity grace and fade, plus expanding rose ripples; glass console with custom gradient range sliders
INTERACTION:
- Moving the hand steers the mesh — particles stream toward the needle, threads tightening around the cursor position
- Clicking empty sky pins a star (with a soft chime if sound is on); dragging a pin drags its neighborhood taut; releasing lets it settle home; double-clicking a pin (or the sky) releases a 26–40 spark shimmer burst with ripple
- Tension slider widens thread reach and stiffness; Density slider rebuilds the field from 30 to 190 particles; Scatter injects radial velocity; Pin 7 seeds a constellation; Sound toggle enables gentle sine chimes on pin and burst
- Live HUD counts threads, pins and sparks four times a second; reduced-motion renders a single calm woven frame
PROMPT:
Build a single-file HTML interactive starfield titled "STARLIGHT LOOM". Background: deep night #040613 to #070b1f with three blurred nebula washes (sky-blue, violet, faint rose) breathing slowly on 29–47s alternating loops under screen blending. Typography: an ultra-thin uppercase title "STARLIGHT LOOM" at clamp(2.6rem–7rem) with 0.32em letter-spacing and a soft violet/blue text-shadow glow, the word LOOM slightly dimmer; above it a mono over-line "NOW WEAVING · INTERACTIVE SKY"; below it an italic ui-serif subtitle explaining the needle metaphor. UI labels are 9–11px ui-monospace uppercase with 0.22–0.5em letter-spacing.

Full-viewport canvas over the washes renders the loom with additive blending. 30–190 drifting particles (density slider) wrap around edges, twinkle with individual phase and speed, and 75% render starlight-white, 25% violet. Particles link to neighbors within ~110px (scaled by tension to ~160px max) with threads whose alpha is quadratic in closeness and whose color grades across the screen from #7dd3fc to #c4b5fd; use a spatial hash so it stays smooth. The cursor is the signature: a bright needle point with a pulsing ring and a 90px radial glow, and particles within 220px are pulled gently toward it, gathering visible threads around the hand.

Clicking empty sky plants a pinned anchor star — a white core with a large violet radial halo and a four-point flare that pops in over 500ms — which threads to every particle within its reach and to every other pin within 340px with thicker, brighter wires. Dragging a pin moves it and pulls connected particles taut; on release it eases back home. Double-clicking a pin (or anywhere) explodes a shimmer burst of 26–40 sparks (white/violet mix) that decelerate with faint gravity, plus an expanding rose ripple ring.

Furniture: top-left brand sigil (starburst in a violet-bordered circle) with "STARLIGHT LOOM / ROOM 07 · WOVEN LIGHT"; top-right a HUD of glass pills showing live thread/pin/spark counts updated 4×/second. Bottom center: a glass pill console (border-radius 999px, backdrop blur, 1px white border) holding two gradient range sliders labeled TENSION and DENSITY, a divider, a "SCATTER" button, a violet "PIN 7" button, and a "SOUND OFF/ON" toggle that enables soft sine chimes (520–680Hz on pin, 880–1100Hz on burst) — audio only after user gesture. Under it, a hint line "NEEDLE FOLLOWS THE HAND · CLICK PINS · DOUBLE-CLICK RELEASES A SHIMMER BURST · DRAG PINS TO PULL THE WEB".

Details: violet-tinted selection, hidden scrollbars, focus rings on all controls, pointer events with touch-action none on the canvas, clicks on the console ignored by the canvas logic. Responsive: title tracking tightens and one HUD pill hides under 640px; console wraps gracefully. prefers-reduced-motion: nebulae stop breathing and the loom renders one composed static frame (still fully informative) instead of a rAF loop. No network, no images — every star, thread and spark is canvas math.
