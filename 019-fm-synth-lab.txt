TITLE: FREQUENCY GARDEN
DESCRIPTION: A boutique FM synthesizer styled as a hand-wound instrument: dark walnut chassis with striped wood grain, cream silkscreened panels with corner screws, LED indicators (vermilion #ff5c39, moss #68d391), an LCD oscilloscope plus log spectrum analyzer, walnut knobs with cream indicators, vertical ADSR faders, a two-octave-split keybed (C4–E5), and two draggable SVG patch cables with real sag curves connecting Mod Out → Car In and Car Out → Filter In. The signature moment: playing the on-screen keys (or A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P) sounds a real two-operator FM voice — carrier/modulator with ratio and index knobs that morph running notes live — while dragging a cable's end to another port re-routes the audio graph itself (FM↔AM bloom, filter in/out) with toast narration and LED confirmation.
TECHNIQUES:
- WebAudio FM voice per note: sine modulator (freq = f × ratio 0.25–8 exponential) through a mod-index gain into carrier frequency param, or re-routed as AM (mod → gain node modulating env.gain); per-voice optional lowpass when the carrier cable ends at Filter In; ADSR via linearRamp attack + setTargetAtTime decay/sustain and release, 8-voice cap
- Draggable patch cables as an absolutely-positioned SVG overlay: cubic Bézier with gravity sag recomputed per pointermove, plug circle riding the free end, invisible 20px stroke-width hit path for grabbing, magnetized snap to nearby valid ports
- Audio graph re-routing on drop: disconnect-less re-plug meaning (FM↔AM mode, filter bypass) applied to future notes and live-updated params of sustaining voices
- LCD aesthetics: Canvas 2D time-domain scope (cream trace, glow) + 32-bar log-sampled spectrum (amber gradient) over scanline overlay; peak-based CLIP/SIG LEDs and tabular mono readouts
- Instrument dressing: repeating-gradient wood grain body, walnut side cheeks, silkscreen brandplate, screw details, LED row with blink, custom range-styled volume, walnut keybed with realistic white/black keys and pointer-glissando
- Accessibility: knobs/faders as role=slider with pointer capture drag + arrow/Home/End keys, aria-live toasts, focus rings, reduced-motion calming
INTERACTION:
- Click "Power · Off" to warm the FG-19 (context resumes, green LED); power off suspends audio
- Play the keybed by pointer (click or drag across keys for glissando) or with the letter row A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P; keys depress and glow vermilion
- Drag the Ratio and Index knobs vertically to morph timbre in real time on sustaining voices (0.25–8 ratio, 0–500 index); Cut the Cutoff knob when the filter is in circuit
- Drag either patch cable's plug: it follows the pointer with sag and snaps to its valid alt port — Mod Out can land on Car In (FM) or VCA In (AM); Car Out lands on Filter In or Mix In; click a port to re-plug without dragging
- ADSR faders shape the envelope per note; volume slider and wave switch (Sine/Tri) finish the voice; LCD readouts track note, voices, ratio, index; CLIP warns on peaks
