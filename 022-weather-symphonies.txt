TITLE: WEATHER, SET TO MUSIC
DESCRIPTION: Four hand-tuned weather scenes — Clear, Rain, Snow, Storm — presented as one continuous ambient composition. A large rounded stage holds four layered SVG landscapes (sun with god-rays over golden hills, slate cloudbanks over blue rain country, powder-blue snowfields with bare trees, and a near-black storm sky) that crossfade over two seconds when the movement changes. A canvas layer paints matching precipitation: slanted rain streaks, swaying snowflakes, or golden pollen drifting in sunlight. A glass plate in the corner names the movement with a poetic serif interlude and Italian tempo marking, while a mono readout tracks air, wind and light as they glide to each sky's values. Below the stage, a performance console of four numbered movement buttons, an intensity slider, and a sound toggle. Sound is fully synthesized: warm triangle pads and air for Clear, filtered noise rain with random sine drips for Rain, a low hush with a trembling high shimmer for Snow, and heavy rain plus LFO-swept wind with brown-noise thunder after each lightning flash for Storm.
TECHNIQUES:
- Four stacked inline SVG scenes sharing one hill geometry (re-colored per weather) with blurred ellipse cloudbanks, radial sun glow, gradient god-ray polygons, and a 2-second opacity crossfade
- Canvas 2D precipitation engine seeded per weather and scaled by the intensity slider: slanted rain streaks with per-drop speed/slant/alpha, sine-swaying snowflakes, drifting pollen motes; DPR-aware, capped counts, rAF loop with cleanup
- WebAudio soundscape built from looping white-noise buffers through per-weather filter chains (lowpass rain, bandpass wind with a 0.09 Hz LFO sweeping center frequency), triangle-oscillator clear-sky pad, random exponential-decay sine drips and bird chirps, and brown-noise thunder swells — all behind a compressor, started only by user toggle
- Two-second simultaneous scene-plus-audio crossfade via linearRampToValueAtTime on each bed's gain
- Storm lightning: randomized flash overlay with double-strike echo, scheduled faster as intensity rises, with brown-noise thunder delayed to follow the flash; CSS custom-property accent color morphs per movement
- Subtle pointer parallax translating the three ground paths at graded depths; glassmorphism plates; tabular-numeral readout eased by its own rAF tween
INTERACTION:
- Click the four movement buttons (or press 1-4 / arrow keys) to crossfade scene and soundscape over two seconds; the poetic plate line swaps with a fade
- Drag the Intensity slider (5-100) to thicken precipitation, raise wind, dim the storm light, quicken lightning, and scale the audio bed level in real time
- Toggle Sound to start the synthesized weather beds and particle-triggered detail sounds (drips, birds, thunder); toggling off ramps the master gain to silence
- Move the pointer across the stage for gentle parallax on the hill layers; hover/focus states on every control; reduced-motion halves particle counts, removes drift animations, and calms flashes
