TITLE: THE HARMONOGRAPH
DESCRIPTION: A Victorian scientific instrument rendered as a working drawing machine. Against a dark walnut workshop wall, a brass-framed machine holds an ivory card under a warm vignette; pressing DRAW sets two small pendulum dials swinging while the pen traces a damped Lissajous figure stroke by stroke in iron-gall ink, sepia, or vermilion. Five brass slider dials (X frequency, Y frequency, damping, phase, pendulum drift) shape each figure; finished works collect below as numbered plates in a horizontal gallery strip, each labelled in roman numerals with a period musical-interval name.
TECHNIQUES:
- Damped Lissajous math (x = sin(wx·t+φ)·e^(−δt), y = sin(wy·t)·e^(−δt)) rendered as thousands of short SVG line segments with stroke width and opacity tied to instantaneous pendulum energy
- Ivory card texture from crossed repeating-linear-gradient grain plus an inset vignette shadow and double keyline border with corner fleurons
- Live pendulum knobs drawn on 46px canvases (brass rod + bob) driven inside the same requestAnimationFrame loop
- Plate thumbnails rasterized by serializing the live SVG to a Blob URL and drawing it into gallery canvases
- Engraved typography: small-caps roman-numeral plate numbers, letterspaced masthead, and a bottom card caption stamped "PLATE"
INTERACTION:
- Drag the five sliders to tune the apparatus; pick one of three ink pots
- Press DRAW (or the D key) to run the twelve-second swing, watching the trace grow; press RANDOMIZE (or R) for wild new figures; mid-draw the button locks until the plate finishes
- Click any finished plate to enlarge it on the card and return its exact parameters to the readout
