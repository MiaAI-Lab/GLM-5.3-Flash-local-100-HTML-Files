TITLE: NEON DISTRICT 2099
DESCRIPTION: A cyberpunk landing page for a fictional streetwear and implant brand, set in pure black and lit only by magenta, cyan and violet signage. A canvas rainstorm leans toward the cursor's x position over a gradient skyline with lit windows; CRT scanlines, a slow brightness sweep and subtle flicker sit over the whole page. The hero holds an ultra-bold condensed headline that idles with occasional twitches and, on hover, shatters into glitch slices with RGB-split cyan and magenta ghost layers. A holographic product card renders a wireframe hi-top sneaker in glowing canvas lines, draggable to spin, beside a monospace spec sheet with animated meters. A marquee ticker, three-column manifesto and mono footer complete the drop.

- RGB-split glitch headline built from stacked absolutely-positioned color layers with clip-path inset keyframes stepping at irregular intervals
- Canvas rain of ~170 streaks whose lean angle eases toward the pointer's normalized x position, with occasional magenta-tinted drops
- Wireframe sneaker modeled as 3D polylines, yaw-rotated, perspective-projected and depth-sorted per segment, stroked with alternating cyan/magenta glow
- CRT atmosphere: repeating-linear-gradient scanlines with overlay blend, animated opacity flicker, a slow full-page light sweep, and radial vignette
- Pure-gradient skyline with masked window-light grids and a holographic card sheen sweep plus corner-bracket decorations
- Infinite marquee ticker via duplicated content and translateX(-50%) loop
INTERACTION:

- Hovering or focusing the headline shatters it into 7 horizontal slices that jitter and skew before reassembling; RGB ghost layers flicker during hover
- Dragging the sneaker canvas spins the wireframe with momentum; releasing it resumes auto-rotation with inertia decay
- Hover states light up nav underlines in neon, spec rows in violet wash, and buttons with intensified glow; full keyboard focus rings and reduced-motion fallbacks throughout
