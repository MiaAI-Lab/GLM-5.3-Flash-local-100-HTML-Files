Magnetic Fridge

Someone bought a bag of words at a flea market in 1994 and the door has never been the same. Drag the words into lines — the magnets will hold them, more or less where you meant.

Snapshot

The camera flashes, then your poem prints in a polaroid frame — caption and all. Keep it on the fridge of your mind.

House Rules

Drag any word anywhere; near an implicit baseline (within 14px) it will snap gently so your lines read true. D scatters the door, F frames the poem, M toggles the hum. A few extra words live in the supply drawer — S spills a handful.

Care & Feeding

The hum is synthesized, not recorded, and only runs while you ask. Defrosting is unnecessary; poetry is self-preserving.

Magnet inventory: forty-one words, four colors, one vowel missing since the move. No photographs were harmed · the flash is hypothetical · the fridge is imaginary
