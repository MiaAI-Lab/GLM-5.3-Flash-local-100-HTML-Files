TITLE: BALLOON POP
DESCRIPTION: A carnival midway dart game under a night sky (#1d2440). A striped red/cream awning with scalloped edges and warm string lights hangs over a barker's banner ("BALLOON POP! — step right up"); below, a plank board holds 21 swaying balloons in multibright colors, some wearing gold star prizes. Every pop is a tiny physics event with synthesized pop noise, milestone fanfares, and toast barks. Type: carnival poster caps with hard red shadows; mono ticket numbers.
