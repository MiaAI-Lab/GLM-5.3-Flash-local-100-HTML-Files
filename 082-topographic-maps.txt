TITLE: Contour
DESCRIPTION: A topographic explorer on survey-office cream. Procedurally generated terrain — a diagonal ridgeline over value-noise hills — is rendered as contour bands (water wash, valley floor, mid slopes, high country) with marchings-squares contour lines, roman-numeral band labels and sun/shade slope tinting. Drawing a route across the map with the pointer instantly builds a vermilion elevation profile beneath, with distance, gain, high point and max-grade readouts; routes that beat the previous high point stamp a named summit into the expedition log. Crosshair hover reads elevation anywhere; New range reseeds a fresh quadrangle.
TECHNIQUES:
- Terrain from three octaves of seeded bilinear value noise plus an analytic diagonal ridge bump and edge falloff to a water rim, all in a 134×134 height field
- Contour lines via marching-squares edge crossing per cell at six levels, drawn per band color; filled bands classified by elevation with per-cell slope shading (lit west faces, shadowed east faces)
- Live elevation profile: route resampled by cumulative distance into up to 220 samples, plotted as a gradient-filled area with meter gridlines, km ticks, a circled high point and its label
- Route facts computed from the same samples — distance (px-to-km map scale), cumulative gain, high point, and max grade over sliding 2% windows
- Expedition stamps: vermilion survey stamps rendered on-canvas at the summit and dashed entries appended to a scrolling log with roman numerals
- Survey furniture: 2px ink borders with hard offset shadows, mono graticule labels, a five-swatch legend, stamped mono panel headers
INTERACTION:
- Drag on the map to draw a route from A to B — the profile chart, distance, gain, high point and grade update stroke by stroke as you draw; a dashed crosshair with an elevation tooltip follows the idle pointer
- Finish a route near (or over) the high point to stamp a summit; each stamp beats the last record to earn its roman-numeral entry
- New range reseeds terrain, renames the quadrangle and graticule, and clears the route; Erase route clears without reseeding; Stamp summits toggles the on-map stamp; Shaded toggles slope shading
- Keyboard-reachable tool buttons with press-down hard-shadow feedback; the profile panel remains empty-state poetic until the first route exists

Header: a 2.5px ink rule under a baseline row — big serif "CONTOUR", and a right meta block ("Quadrangle: <generated name> · Series: Gallery of 100", "Contour interval: 120 m · Draw a route with your pointer").

Main bench (map + 230–320px side column): the map is a bordered canvas (2px ink border, 6px offset shadow, clamp 340–620px tall) rendering a procedural height field as filled contour bands — water below 0.14, then four land bands — with marching-squares contour lines stroked in each band's line color, roman numerals I–V scattered on their bands, and optional per-cell slope shading (pale highlight on up-slope faces, dark tint on down-slope). A summit triangle marks the highest cell. Mono corner labels ("N 46°12′", "SCALE 1 : 50 000") and a bordered legend card (Water / Valley floor / Mid slopes / High country / Your route) sit in the corners.

Side column panels (Field kit, Route log, Route facts): hard-shadowed bordered cards on cream. Field kit holds four press-down buttons — NEW RANGE, ERASE ROUTE, STAMP SUMMITS, SHADED: ON (the last toggling slope shading, the first reseeding). Route log is a dashed-border list that fills with stamped summits — roman numeral, italic serif name ("Mount Serena", "Cairn Vesper"), mono elevation — newest first, each landing with a scale-rotate stamp-in animation, plus an empty-state line "No summits stamped yet — the peaks are still waiting to be named." Route facts: four bordered cells reading Distance (km), Gain (m), High point (m), Grade (max %).

Signature interaction: drag anywhere on the map to draw a vermilion survey route with round joins and A/B endpoint dots; the moment you start drawing, the elevation profile panel beneath comes alive — a red line rising and falling over a cream-to-red gradient area, meter gridlines and km ticks annotated in mono, the route's high point circled and labeled ("1843 m"), and a live readout ("now 1204 m · high 1843 m"). Distance, gain, high point and max grade update continuously while drawing. If a finished route's high point beats the previous record, a rotated vermilion stamp "BM 1843" appears on the map summit and a named entry lands in the Route log.

Hovering the map shows a dashed crosshair with a boxed elevation tooltip ("1487 m"). New range reseeds the terrain (new ridge shape, water rim, summit), generates a new quadrangle name and graticule from seeded word lists, clears the route and log stamps' map marks but keeps the log. Responsive: side column wraps under the map under 900px with panels side-by-side; map and profile heights clamp fluidly. Reduced motion: stamp animations removed, everything else instant. No network, no images — the land is math on canvas.
