TITLE: MAGNETIC FRIDGE
DESCRIPTION: A kitchen fridge door as a poem machine. Forty-one glossy word tiles — serif, sans and mono, each ringed by a colored magnet border with a round magnet on its underside — are scattered across brushed appliance white. Words drag anywhere with a gentle magnetic snap onto implicit 56px baselines so lines read true. Snapshot mode flashes, sounds a shutter, and prints your current arrangement inside a polaroid frame with a tape strip and a mono caption of your poem. Optional synthesized fridge hum (60/120 Hz with a warbling triangle overtone), a scatter button, an extra-words drawer, and a brushed-steel finish toggle.
TECHNIQUES:
- Appliance realism: layered gradients with inset white highlights and door-seam shadow; brushed-steel mode adds fine vertical repeating stripes
- Word tiles with CSS magnet: colored border edge, radial-gradient magnet dot positioned below, ink variation classes (rotation, tracking, size) per tile
- Baseline snapping: dragging finds the nearest implicit line within 14px and glues the tile's y to it
- Polaroid print: cloned tiles rendered into a mini fridge shot with percentage positions, flash overlay animation, shutter/click WebAudio blips, and a caption assembled by sorting tiles into reading lines
- Fridge hum: user-initiated oscillators (60 Hz sine, 120 Hz sine, detuned 179.8 Hz triangle through lowpass) with slow fade-in and gain-ramped stop
INTERACTION:
- Drag any word anywhere; it snaps to the nearest poetic baseline; arrow keys nudge tiles when focused
- "Frame the Poem" (or F key) triggers flash + shutter and opens the polaroid with your poem captioned; click outside or press Escape to put it back
- "Scatter" (D), "More Words" (S) spill tiles; "Fridge Hum: On" (M) starts the drone; "Brushed Steel" switches the finish
