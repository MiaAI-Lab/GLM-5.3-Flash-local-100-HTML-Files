TITLE: MERIDIAN
DESCRIPTION: A glassmorphic weather-and-time console over three crossfading skies. A hero dial traces a semicircular sun/moon arc: the amber (or sakura, or terracotta) sun rides the arch from sunrise to sunset at its true hourly position, and a pearl moon takes the night shift — the progress arc, drawn in the city accent, advances with your actual local clock. Beside it, a giant thin-numeral clock, an italic dateline, condition line and high/low chips; a 24-hour forecast ring of radial temperature spokes labelled every three hours; four frosted metric cards (wind, humidity, pressure, visibility) with canvas sparklines and 24-hour trends; a serif italic sky-notes panel. Switching Reykjavík / Kyoto / Marrakech morphs every colour variable, the sky gradient, the sun colour, the sparklines and the copy in one soft crossfade.
TECHNIQUES:
- Three fixed sky layers crossfaded by body-class (ice blue, sakura pink, terracotta) with blurred drifting cloud blobs and a per-city sun bleed; CSS custom properties (--ink/--accent) retint all text and chrome
- SVG sun/moon arc dial: 12 tick marks, a day-progress arc built from a large-arc-flag path helper, and marker groups translated along the semicircle by hour-interpolated progress; moon cut-out drawn as an offset circle
- Hourly ring of 24 radial spokes whose lengths map each hour's temperature between min/max, with hour labels every 3 hours, an accent "now" spoke and a now-dot; rebuilt per minute
- Canvas sparklines per metric: 24-point line + gradient fill, accent-tinted, redrawing during the 0.9 s accent lerp so the colour morphs smoothly; DOM values swap per city with true 24 h trends
INTERACTION:
- City switcher morphs palettes, skies, sun colour, dial, ring, sparklines and copy with a 1.2 s crossfade; the clock always shows your real local time
- The dial and progress arc advance continuously with the clock; the temperature, metric values and ring now-marker refresh each minute
- Everything readable at 360px: single-column reflow, wrapping city pills, sparklines resize on window resize; reduced motion freezes clouds and snaps the accent
