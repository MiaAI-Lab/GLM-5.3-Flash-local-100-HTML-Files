TITLE: ROSE WINDOW
DESCRIPTION: The great gothic rose of a cathedral transept, rebuilt as a living light instrument. A full radial SVG window — twenty-four apostle wedges, a stone band of quatrefoils, twelve virtue lancets with pointed tops, an inner ring of planets and weathers, and an amber medallion heart — all held in heavy black lead tracery inside a carved stone disc. A draggable sun orbits the window; as it arcs, every pane's illumination is recomputed from its facing angle, so jewel tones deepen, flare white where they catch the light, and finally die to near-black at night. A candle mode ignites the window from within, and a canonical-hours readout tracks Lauds through Compline.
TECHNIQUES:
- Procedural SVG architecture: wedge and petal paths generated from polar math, layered glass/shade/flare paths per pane, all lead lines drawn as explicit strokes
- Lighting engine: per-pane shade opacity computed from cosine of (pane azimuth − sun azimuth) times solar elevation, with white radial-gradient flares when facing exceeds 0.9
- Sky gradient behind the stone driven by an interpolated eleven-color day-cycle palette exposed through CSS custom properties
- Gothic inscription ring on a circular textPath in small-caps Latin, plus roman-numeral hour and named office readouts
- Candle mode: radial falloff from the medallion (rFrac weight) with a slow double-sine flicker, shifting shades from cold night blue to warm ember brown
INTERACTION:
- Drag anywhere in the stonework's orbit to move the sun; the readout, sky, and every pane respond in real time
- Keyboard: focus the sun handle or the brass knob and use arrow keys (shift for coarse steps)
- Hover any pane to read its dedication — apostles, virtues, planets, weathers — in the caption below
- "Let the Day Pass" sets the sun moving forever; "Candlelight" toggles the inner glow with animated flicker
PROMPT:
Design ROSE WINDOW: a dark cathedral page whose hero is a huge circular gothic rose window, drawn entirely in SVG against a night sky that changes color with the hour. The window is centered, roughly 860px wide, and structurally authentic: a stone backing disc (#9a948a) with carved ring bands; an outer ring of 24 glass wedges (radius 336–462) in alternating cobalt #274690, ruby #8e2c3a, emerald #2e6e4e and occasional amber #d9a441 or pale white glass; a stone band containing a ring of small quatrefoil bosses; a middle ring of 12 pointed lancet petals in emerald, violet, amber, cobalt and ruby; an inner ring of 12 offset wedges; a six-pane rosette; and a central amber medallion. Over everything lies thick black (#1a1a1a) lead tracery: concentric bands, radial cames between every wedge, crosses at the petal tips. Around the outside, a circular inscription ring in small-caps Latin runs on a textPath: "lux in tenebris lucet · domus est illuminatio mea…". Each glass pane uses a radial gradient offset toward its upper-left (highlight 38%/34%), and carries two invisible overlays: a darkness shade and a white flare gradient.

The signature interaction: a golden sun disc with twelve rays floats on an orbit outside the window's top, attached to a soft halo. Dragging the pointer around the window (pointer events with capture; pokes inside the glass ring are ignored) sets the hour of day. The lighting engine recomputes each pane: shade opacity = 1 − ambient × (0.34 + 0.66 × max(0, cos(paneAngle − sunAngle))), where ambient rises with solar elevation; panes facing the sun within 0.9 cosine flare with the white gradient up to 0.32 opacity. The page background sky interpolates through midnight navy, dawn rose, noon blue, dusk copper and back. Below the window, a caption shows the current dedication in amber small caps with an italic English virtue, and a stone plinth panel holds a monospace hour readout ("Hour III · 15:00"), the canonical office name (Lauds, Prime, Sext, None, Vespers, Compline), a horizontal day-cycle slider track with tick dots and a glowing brass knob, and two stone buttons: "Let the Day Pass" (an endless slow solar animation via requestAnimationFrame, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion) and "Candlelight" — a pressed amber state that reverses the shading into a warm inner glow: brightness weighted toward the medallion, ember-brown shades, slow double-sine flicker.

The whole piece should feel like standing in a cold transept at dusk, watching the last light walk across twelve hundred pieces of glass.
