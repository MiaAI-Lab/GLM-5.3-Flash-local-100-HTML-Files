Paeonia lactiflora · studiō fol. 13 r

Observations

Painted from the studio window on a morning of thin light. The peony was chosen for its dishonesty — a flower that pretends to be one bloom and is actually fifteen nested arguments, each petal a small revision of the last.

The ferns keep the peace along the stem, thirteen leaflets to a frond, drawn quickly before the light turned.

The watering can

dry pigment — safe to touch

Watering re-wets the wash: colors deepen and bleed toward one another, the way the morning actually felt. Touch the blossom to see the petals lift.
