TITLE: Bauhaus Bounce
DESCRIPTION: A physics toy in Bauhaus language: a poster header with a giant numeral 09 and primary-color wordmark sits above a full-height paper-grid yard where red circles, blue bars and yellow triangles drop, bounce and stack under verlet physics, each casting a hard offset shadow. Shapes spawn from clicks, can be grabbed and flung with a rubber-band drag, and rest on a hatched floor line. A bottom console offers spawn chips with color swatches, a gravity slider from 0.10 to 2.20 g, a Sweep button, and the signature Compose button that arranges a balanced primary-color still-life; a toast announces each action.
TECHNIQUES:
- Verlet integration point-mass bodies: circles as 12-gon rims with radial cross-bracing and a hub point, bars as cross-braced rectangles, triangles as rigid 3-point frames, each with 5 constraint iterations per frame
- Shape-vs-shape collision via bounding-box pruning and segment push-out with padding, plus floor/wall restitution applied through position-memory reflection
- Hard-offset Bauhaus shadows drawn in canvas: a black underlayer offset 4–5px behind the color face, plus soft ellipse contact shadows on the floor that shrink with fall height
- Physics-driven rendering: radius and rotation are read back from the simulated point cloud, so circles visibly roll and bars tip realistically
- Poster UI: 4px black borders, hard 4px/5px offset chip shadows with press-down active states, CSS-drawn spawn swatches (circle, rectangle, triangle), warm paper grid from layered linear gradients
- Gravity slider (0.10–2.20 g) rescaling a 900 px/s² base; toasts for Sweep and Compose actions
INTERACTION:
- Clicking empty yard space spawns a random shape at that point; the three spawn chips drop circles, bars or triangles from above
- Grabbing any shape attaches a soft rubber-band to the nearest point — drag slowly to position, whip to fling with momentum; a dashed tether shows the pull
- The gravity slider changes the whole yard's temperament live, from drifting balloon-light to brutal 2.2 g slams
- Compose clears the yard and drops a choreographed still-life — yellow triangle, blue bar, red circles and a black bar — that settles into balance; Sweep wipes everything; piece counter caps at 60
