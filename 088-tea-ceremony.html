warm water waiting · the kettle is quiet

Step 01 · 4 steps of 4bowl o-atsu

Warm the Bowl

Hot water from the kettle, swirled once, poured away. The clay learns the temperature of the tea it is about to hold.

Step 02matcha o-ireru

Add the Matcha

Two scoops of the green — the season's first grinding, whisked from a tin older than the house.

Step 03chasen o-awaseru

Whisk, and Wait

Move your pointer over the bowl — quick wrist strokes, back and forth, never circles. The froth tells you when to stop.

Step 04o-cha o-dozo

Serve, and Bow

The bowl turns twice in your hands before it leaves you. One encounter, one chance — ichigo ichie.
